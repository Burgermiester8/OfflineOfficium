109:1 THE LORD said únto my Lórd, * Sit thou on my right hand, until I make thine enemiés thy foótstool.
109:2 The LORD shall send the rod of thy power oút of Síon: * be thou ruler, even in the midst among thine énemiés.
109:3 In the day of thy power shall thy people offer themselves willingly with an hóly wórship: * thy young men come to thee as dew from the wómb of the mórning.
109:4 The LORD sware, and will nót repént, * Thou art a Priest forever after the order of Melchízedék.
109:5 The Lord upón thy right hánd * shall wound even kings in the dáy of his wráth.
109:6 He shall judge amóng the heáthen; * he shall fill the places with the dead bodies, † and smite in sunder the heads over dívers coúntries.
109:7 He shall drink of the broók in the wáy; * therefore shall he lift úp his heád.
