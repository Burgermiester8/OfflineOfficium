50:3 HAVE mercy upon me, O God, after thy great goodness; * according to the multitude of thy mercies do away mine offences.
50:4 Wash me throughly from my wickedness, * and cleanse me from my sin.
50:5 For I acknowledge my faults, * and my sin is ever before me.
50:6 Against thee only have I sinned, and done this evil in thy sight; * that thou mightest be justified in thy saying, and clear when thou shalt judge.
50:7 Behold, I was shapen in wickedness, * and in sin hath my mother conceived me.
50:8 But lo, thou requirest truth in the inward parts, * and shalt make me to understand wisdom secretly.
50:9 Thou shalt purge me with hyssop, and I shall be clean; * thou shalt wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow.
50:10 Thou shalt make me hear of joy and gladness, * that the bones which thou hast broken may rejoice.
50:11 Turn thy face from my sins, * and put out all my misdeeds.
50:12 Make me a clean heart, O God, * and renew a right spirit within me.
50:13 Cast me not away from thy presence, * and take not thy holy Spirit from me.
50:14 O give me the comfort of thy help again, * and stablish me with thy free Spirit.
50:15 Then shall I teach thy ways unto the wicked, * and sinners shall be converted unto thee.
50:16 Deliver me from blood-guiltiness, O God, thou that art the God of my health; * and my tongue shall sing of thy righteousness.
50:17 Thou shalt open my lips, O Lord, * and my mouth shall show thy praise.
50:18 For thou desirest no sacrifice, else would I give it thee; * but thou delightest not in burnt-offerings.
50:19 The sacrifice of God is a troubled spirit: * a broken and contrite heart, O God, shalt thou not despise.
50:20 O be favorable and gracious unto Sion; * build thou the walls of Jerusalem.
50:21 Then shalt thou be pleased with the sacrifice of righteousness, with the burnt-offerings and oblations; * then shall they offer young bullocks upon thine altar.
