46:2 O CLAP your hands together, all ye peoples: * O sing unto God with the voice of melody.
46:3 For the LORD is high, and to be feared; * he is the great King upon all the earth.
46:4 He shall subdue the peoples under us, * and the nations under our feet.
46:5 He shall choose out an heritage for us, * even the excellency of Jacob, whom he loved.
46:6 God is gone up with a merry noise, * and the LORD with the sound of the trump.
46:7 O sing praises, sing praises unto our God; * O sing praises, sing praises unto our King.
46:8 For God is the King of all the earth: * sing ye praises with understanding.
46:9 God reigneth over the nations; * God sitteth upon his holy seat.
46:10 The princes of the peoples are joined unto the people of the God of Abraham; * for God, which is very high exalted, doth defend the earth, as it were with a shield.
