48:2 O HEAR ye this, all ye people; * ponder it with your ears, all ye that dwell in the world;
48:3 High and low, rich and poor, * one with another.
48:4 My mouth shall speak of wisdom, * and my heart shall muse of understanding.
48:5 I will incline mine ear to the parable, * and show my dark speech upon the harp.
48:6 Wherefore should I fear in the days of evil, * when wickedness at my heels compasseth me round about?
48:7 There be some that put their trust in their goods, * and boast themselves in the multitude of their riches.
48:8a But no man may deliver his brother, * nor give a ransom unto God for him,
48:8b For it cost more to redeem their souls, * so that he must let that alone forever;
48:9 That he shall live alway, * and not see the grave.
48:11 For he seeth that wise men also die and perish together, * as well as the ignorant and foolish, and leave their riches for other.
48:12 And yet they think that their houses shall continue forever, and that their dwelling-places shall endure from one generation to another; * and call the lands after their own names.
48:13 Nevertheless, man being in honor abideth not, * seeing he may be compared unto the beasts that perish;
48:14 This their way is very foolishness; * yet their posterity praise their saying.
48:15 They lie in the grave like sheep; death is their shepherd; and the righteous shall have dominion over them in the morning: * their beauty shall consume in the sepulchre, and have no abiding.
48:16 But God hath delivered my soul from the power of the grave; * for he shall receive me.
48:17 Be not thou afraid, though one be made rich, * or if the glory of his house be increased;
48:18 For he shall carry nothing away with him when he dieth, * neither shall his pomp follow him.
48:19 For while he lived, he counted himself an happy man; * and so long as thou doest well unto thyself, men will speak good of thee.
48:20 He shall follow the generation of his fathers, * and shall never see light.
48:21 Man that is in honor but hath no understanding * is compared unto the beasts that perish.
