129:1 OUT of the deep have I called unto theé, O Lórd; * Lord, heár my voíce.
129:2b O let thine ears consíder wéll * the voice of mý complaínt.
129:3 If thou, Lord, wilt be extreme to mark what is dóne amíss, * O Lord, whó may abíde it?
129:4a For there is mércy with theé; * therefore shalt thóu be féared.
129:4b I look for the Lord; my soul doth waít for hím; * in his wórd is my trúst.
129:6 My soul fleeth unto the Lord before the mórning wátch; * I say, before the mórning wátch.
129:7 O Israel, trust in the Lord; for with the Lórd there is mércy, * and with him is plénteous redémption.
129:8 And he shall redeem Ísraél * from áll his síns.
