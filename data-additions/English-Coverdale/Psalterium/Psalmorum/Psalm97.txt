97:1 O SING unto the LORD a new song; * for he hath done marvelous things.
97:1 With his own right hand, and with his holy arm, * hath he gotten himself the victory.
97:2 The LORD declared his salvation; * his righteousness hath he openly showed in the sight of the heathen.
97:3 He hath remembered his mercy and truth toward the house of Israel; * and all the ends of the world have seen the salvation of our God.
97:4 Show yourselves joyful unto the LORD, all ye lands; * sing, rejoice, and give thanks.
97:5 Praise the LORD upon the harp; * sing to the harp with a psalm of thanksgiving.
97:6a With trumpets also and shawms, * O show yourselves joyful before the LORD, the King.
97:6b Let the sea make a noise, and all that therein is; * the round world, and they that dwell therein.
97:8 Let the floods clap their hands, and let the hills be joyful together before the LORD; * for he is come to judge the earth.
97:9 With righteousness shall he judge the world, * and the peoples with equity.
