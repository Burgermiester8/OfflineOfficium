142:1 HEAR my prayer, O LORD, and consíder my desíre; * harken unto me for thy truth and ríghteousness’ sáke.
142:2 And enter not into júdgment with thy sérvant; * for in thy sight shall no man líving be jústified.
142:3a For the enemy hath persecuted my soul; † he hath smitten my life dówn to the gróund; * he hath laid me in the darkness, as the men that have béen long déad.
142:3b Therefore is my spirit véxed withín me, * and my heart withín me is désolate.
142:5 Yet do I remember the time past; † I muse upon áll thy wórks; * yea, I exercise myself in the wórks of thy hánds.
142:6 I stretch forth my hánds unto thée; * my soul gaspeth unto thee as a thírsty lánd.
142:7 Hear me, O LORD, and that soon; † for my spirit wáxeth fáint: * hide not thy face from me, lest I be like unto them that go dówn into the pít.
142:8 O let me hear thy loving-kindness betimes in the morning; † for in thée is my trúst: * show thou me the way that I should walk in; † for I lift up my sóul unto thée.
142:9 Deliver me, O LÓRD, from mine enémies; * for I flee unto thée to híde me.
142:10b Teach me to do the thing that pleaseth thee; for thóu art my Gód: * let thy loving Spirit lead me forth into the lánd of ríghteousness.
142:11b Quicken me, O LÓRD, for thy Náme’s sake; * and for thy righteousness’ sake bring my sóul out of tróuble.
142:12b And of thy goodness sláy mine énemies, * and destroy all them that vex my soul; for Í am thy sérvant.
