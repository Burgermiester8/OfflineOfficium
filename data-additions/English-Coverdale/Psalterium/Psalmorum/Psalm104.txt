104:1 O GIVE thanks unto the LORD, and call upón his Náme; * tell the people what thíngs he hath dóne.
104:2 O let your songs be of hím, and praíse him; * and let your talking be of all his wóndrous wórks.
104:3 Rejoice in his hóly Náme; * let the heart of them rejoice that seék the LÓRD.
104:4 Seek the LÓRD and his stréngth; * seek his fáce evermóre.
104:5 Remember the marvelous works that hé hath dóne; * his wonders, and the judgments óf his moúth;
104:6 O ye seed of Abrahám his sérvant, * ye children of Jácob his chósen.
104:7 He is the LÓRD our Gód; * his judgments are in áll the wórld.
104:8 He hath been alway mindful of his covenánt and prómise, * that he made to a thousand génerátions;
104:9 Even the covenant that he made with Ábrahám; * and the oath that he swáre unto Ísaac;
104:10 And appointed the same unto Jacob fór a láw, * and to Israel for an everlasting téstamént;
104:11 Saying, Unto thee will I give the lánd of Cánaan, * the lot of your inhéritánce:
104:12 When there were yet but a féw of thém, * and they strangers ín the lánd;
104:13 What time as they went from one nation tó anóther, * from one kingdom to anóther peóple;
104:14 He suffered no man to dó them wróng, * but reproved even kíngs for their sákes;
104:15 Touch not míne anoínted, * and do my próphets no hárm.
104:16 Moreover, he called for a dearth upón the lánd, * and destroyed all the provísion of breád.
104:17 But he had sent a mán befóre them, * even Joseph, who was sold to bé a bónd-servant;
104:18a Whose feet they húrt in the stócks; * the iron entered ínto his sóul;
104:18b Until the time came that his caúse was knówn: * the word of the LÓRD tríed him.
104:19 The king sent, and delívered hím; * the prince of the people lét him go freé.
104:21 He made him lord also óf his hoúse, * and ruler of áll his súbstance;
104:22 That he might inform his princes áfter his wíll, * and teach his sénators wísdom.
104:23 ISRAEL also came ínto Égypt, * and Jacob was a stranger in the lánd of Hám.
104:24 And he increased his people exceédinglÿ, * and made them stronger than their énemíes;
104:25 Whose heart turned so, that they háted his peóple, * and dealt untruly wíth his sérvants.
104:26 Then sent he Móses his sérvant, * and Aaron whom hé had chósen.
104:27 And these showed his tókens amóng them, * and wonders in the lánd of Hám.
104:28 He sent darkness, and ít was dárk; * and they were not obedient untó his wórd.
104:29 He turned their waters ínto bloód, * and sléw their físh.
104:30 Their land broúght forth frógs; * yea, even ín their kings’ chámbers.
104:31 He spake the word, and there came all mánner of fliés, * and lice in áll their quárters.
104:32 He gave them haílstones for raín; * and flames of fire ín their lánd.
104:33 He smote their vines álso and fíg trees; * and destroyed the trees that were ín their coásts.
104:34 He spake the word, and the grasshoppers came, † and caterpillars innúmerablé, * and did eat up all the grass in their land, † and devoured the fruít of their groúnd.
104:36 He smote all the firstborn ín their lánd; * even the chief of áll their stréngth.
104:37 He brought them forth also with sílver and góld; * there was not one feeble person amóng their tríbes.
104:38 Egypt was glad at theír depárting; * for they were afraíd of thém.
104:39 He spread out a cloud to be a cóveríng, * and fire to give light ín the night seáson.
104:40 At their desíre he brought quaíls; * and he filled them with the breád of heáven.
104:41 He opened the rock of stone, and the wáters flowed oút, * so that rivers ran ín the dry pláces.
104:42 For why? he remembered his hóly prómise; * and Abrahám his sérvant.
104:43 And he brought forth his peóple with jóy, * and his chósen with gládness;
104:44 And gave them the lánds of the heáthen; * and they took the labors of the people ín posséssion;
104:45 That they might keép his státutes, * and obsérve his láws.
