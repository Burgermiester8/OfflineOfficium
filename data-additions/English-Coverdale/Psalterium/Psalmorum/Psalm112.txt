112:1 PRAISE the LÓRD, ye sérvants; * O praise the Náme of the LÓRD.
112:2 Blessed be the Náme of the LÓRD * from this time forth forévermóre.
112:3 The LORD’s Náme is praísed * from the rising up of the sun unto the going dówn of the sáme.
112:4 The LORD is high abóve all nátions, * and his glory abóve the heávens.
112:5 Who is like unto the LORD our God, that hath his dwélling so hígh, * and yet humbleth himself to behold the things that are in heáven and eárth!
112:7 He taketh up the simple oút of the dúst, * and lifteth the poor oút of the míre;
112:8 That he may set him wíth the prínces, * even with the princes óf his peóple.
112:9 He maketh the barren woman tó keep hoúse, * and to be a joyful móther of chíldren.
