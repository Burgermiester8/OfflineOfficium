4:2 HEAR me when I call, O God of my righteousness: † thou hast set me at liberty when Ī was in tróuble; * have mercy upon me, and hárken unto my práyer.
4:3 O ye sons of men, how long will ye bláspheme mine hónor, * and have such pleasure in vanity, and séek after fálsehood?
4:4 Know this also, that the LORD hath chosen to himself the mán that is gódly; * when I call upon the LÓRD he will héar me.
4:5 Stand in áwe, and sin nót; * commune with your own heart, and in your chámber, and be stíll.
4:6a Offer the sácrifice of ríghteousness, * and put your trúst in the LÓRD.
4:6b There be mány that sáy, * Who will shów us any góod?
4:7 LORD, líft thou úp * the light of thy cóuntenance upón us.
4:8 Thou hast put gládness in my héart; * yea, more than when their corn and wine and óil incréase.
4:9 I will lay me down in peace, and táke my rést; * for it is thou, LORD, only, that makest me dwéll in sáfety.
