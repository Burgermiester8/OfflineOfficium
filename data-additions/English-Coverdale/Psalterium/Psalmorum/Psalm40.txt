40:2 BLESSED is he that consídereth the póor and néedy; * the LORD shall delíver him in the tíme of tróuble.
40:3 The LORD preserve him, and keep him alive, that hé may be bléssed upon éarth; * and deliver not thou hím into the wíll of his énemies.
40:4 The LORD comfort him when he lieth síck upón his béd; * make thou áll his béd in his síckness.
40:5 I said, LORD, bé mérciful únto me; * heal my soul, for Í have sínned agáinst thee.
40:6 Mine enemies spéak évil of me, * When shall he díe, and his náme pérish?
40:7 And if he come to sée me, he spéaketh vánity, * and his heart conceiveth falsehood within himself; and when he cómeth forth, he télleth it.
40:8 All mine enemies whísper togéther agáinst me; * even against me do théy imágine this évil.
40:9 An evil disease, say they, cléaveth fást únto him; * and now that he lieth, he shall ríse úp no móre.
40:10 Yea, even mine own famíliar friénd whom I trústed, * who did also eat of my bread, hath láid great wáit for mé.
40:11 But be thou mérciful únto me, O LÓRD; * raise thou me up agáin, and Í shall rewárd them.
40:12 By this I knów thou fávorest me, * that mine enemy dóth not tríumph agáinst me.
40:13 And in my innocency thóu uphóldest mé, * and shalt set me befóre thy fáce foréver.
40:14 Blessed be the LÓRD Gód of Ísrael, * wórld withóut end. Amén.
