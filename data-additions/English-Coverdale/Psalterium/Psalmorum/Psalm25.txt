25:1 BE thou my Judge, O LORD, for I háve walked ínnocently: * my trust hath been also in the LORD, therefore shall Í not fáll.
25:2 Examine me, O LÓRD, and próve me; * try out my réins and my héart.
25:3 For thy loving-kindness is ever befóre mine éyes; * and I will wálk in thy trúth.
25:4 I have not dwélt with vain pérsons; * neither will I have fellowship wíth the decéitful.
25:5 I have hated the congregátion of the wícked; * and will not sit amóng the ungódly.
25:6 I will wash my hands in ínnocency, O LÓRD; * and so will I gó to thine áltar;
25:7 That I may show the vóice of thanksgíving, * and tell of all thy wóndrous wórks.
25:8 LORD, I have loved the habitátion of thy hóuse, * and the place where thine hónor dwélleth.
25:9 O shut not up my sóul with the sínners, * nor my lífe with the blóodthirsty;
25:10 In whose hánds is wíckedness, * and their right hand is fúll of gífts.
25:11 But as for me, I wíll walk ínnocently: * O deliver me, and be mérciful unto mé.
25:12 My foot stándeth ríght: * I will praise the LÓRD in the congregátions.
