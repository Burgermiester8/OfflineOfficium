91:2 IT is a good thing to give thanks unto the LORD, * and to sing praises unto thy Name, O Most Highest;
91:3 To tell of thy loving-kindness early in the morning, * and of thy truth in the night season;
91:4 Upon an instrument of ten strings, and upon the lute; * upon a loud instrument, and upon the harp.
91:5 For thou, LORD, hast made me glad through thy works; * and I will rejoice in giving praise for the operations of thy hands.
91:6 O LORD, how glorious are thy works! * thy thoughts are very deep.
91:7 An unwise man doth not well consider this, * and a fool doth not understand it.
91:8 When the ungodly are green as the grass, and when all the workers of wickedness do flourish, * then shall they be destroyed forever; but thou, LORD, art the Most Highest forevermore.
91:10 For lo, thine enemies, O LORD, lo, thine enemies shall perish; * and all the workers of wickedness shall be destroyed.
91:11 But my horn shall be exalted like the horn of an unicorn; * for I am anointed with fresh oil.
91:12 Mine eye also shall see his lust of mine enemies, * and mine ear shall hear his desire of the wicked that arise up against me.
91:13 The righteous shall flourish like a palm tree, * and shall spread abroad like a cedar in Lebanon.
91:14 Such as are planted in the house of the LORD, * shall flourish in the courts of the house of our God.
91:15 They also shall bring forth more fruit in their age, * and shall be fat and well-liking;
91:16 That they may show how true the LORD my strength is, * and that there is no unrighteousness in him.
