111:1 BLESSED is the man that feáreth the LÓRD; * he hath great delight in hís commándments.
111:2 His seed shall be míghty upon eárth; * the generation of the faithful sháll be bléssed.
111:3 Riches and plenteousness shall be ín his hoúse; * and his righteousness endúreth foréver.
111:4 Unto the godly there ariseth up light ín the dárkness; * he is merciful, lóving, and ríghteous.
111:5 A good man is mercifúl, and léndeth; * and will guide his words wíth discrétion.
111:6 For he shall néver be móved: * and the righteous shall be had in everlásting remémbrance.
111:7a He will not be afraid of any évil tídings; * for his heart standeth fast, and believeth ín the LÓRD.
111:7b His heart is stablished, and wíll not shrínk, * until he see his desire upon his énemiés.
111:9 He hath dispersed abroad, and given tó the poór. * and his righteousness remaineth forever; † his horn shall be exálted with hónor.
111:10 The ungodly shall see it, and ít shall griéve him; * he shall gnash with his teeth, and consume away; † the desire of the ungódly shall pérish.
