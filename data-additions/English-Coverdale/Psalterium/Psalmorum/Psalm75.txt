75:2 IN Júdah is God knówn; * his Name is gréat in Ísrael.
75:3 At Sálem is his tábernacle, * and his dwélling in Síon.
75:4 There brake he the árrows of the bów, * the shield, the swórd, and the báttle.
75:5 Thou art glórious in míght, * when thou comest from the hílls of the róbbers.
75:6 The proud are robbed, they have slépt their sléep; * and all the men whose hands were míghty have found nóthing.
75:7 At thy rebuke, O Gód of Jácob, * both the chariot and hórse are fállen.
75:8 Thou, even thóu art to be féared; * and who may stand in thy sight when thóu art ángry?
75:9 Thou didst cause thy judgment to be héard from héaven; * the earth trémbled, and was stíll,
75:10 When God aróse to júdgment, * and to help all the méek upon éarth.
75:11 The fierceness of man shall túrn to thy práise; * and the fierceness of them shalt thóu refráin.
75:12 Promise unto the LORD your God, and keep it, all ye that are róund abóut him; * bring presents unto him that óught to be féared.
75:13 He shall refrain the spírit of prínces, * and is wonderful among the kíngs of the éarth.
