127:1 BLESSED are all they that féar the Lórd, * and wálk in his wáys.
127:2 For thou shalt eat the lábors of thine hánds: * O well is thee, and háppy shalt thou bé.
127:3a Thy wife shall be as the fruítful víne * upon the wálls of thine hóuse;
127:3b Thy children like the ólive bránches * round aboút thy táble.
127:4 Lo, thus shall the mán be bléssed * that feáreth the Lórd.
127:5 The Lord from out of Síon shall so bléss thee, * that thou shalt see Jerusalem in prosperity áll thy life lóng;
127:6 Yea, that thou shalt see thy chíldren’s chíldren, * and péace upon Ísrael.
