47:2 GREAT is the LORD, and highly to be praised * in the city of our God, even upon his holy hill.
47:3 The hill of Sion is a fair place, and the joy of the whole earth; * upon the north side lieth the city of the great King: God is well known in her palaces as a sure refuge.
47:5 For lo, the kings of the earth * were gathered, and gone by together.
47:6 They marvelled to see such things; * they were astonished, and suddenly cast down.
47:7a Fear came there upon them; and sorrow, * as upon a woman in her travail.
47:7b Thou dost break the ships of the sea * through the east-wind.
47:9 Like as we have heard, so have we seen in the city of the LORD of hosts, in the city of our God; * God upholdeth the same forever.
47:10 We wait for thy loving-kindness, O God, * in the midst of thy temple.
47:11 O God, according to thy Name, so is thy praise unto the world’s end; * thy right hand is full of righteousness.
47:12 Let the mount Sion rejoice, and the daughters of Judah be glad, * because of thy judgments.
47:13 Walk about Sion, and go round about her; * and tell the towers thereof.
47:14 Mark well her bulwarks, consider her palaces, * that ye may tell them that come after.
47:15 For this God is our God for ever and ever: * he shall be our guide unto death.
