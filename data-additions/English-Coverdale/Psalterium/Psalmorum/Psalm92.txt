92:1a THE LORD is King, and hath put on glorious apparel; * the LORD hath put on his apparel, and girded himself with strength.
92:1b He hath made the round world so sure, * that it cannot be moved.
92:2 Ever since the world began, hath thy seat been prepared: * thou art from everlasting.
92:3 The floods are risen, O LORD, the floods have lift up their voice; * the floods lift up their waves.
92:4b The waves of the sea are mighty, and rage horribly; * but yet the LORD, who dwelleth on high, is mightier.
92:5 Thy testimonies, O LORD, are very sure: * holiness becometh thine house forever.
