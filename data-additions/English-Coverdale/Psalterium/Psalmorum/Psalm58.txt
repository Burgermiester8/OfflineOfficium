58:7 They go to and fró in the évening, * they grin like a dog, and run about thróugh the cíty.

58:10 My strength will I áscribe unto thée; * for thou art the Gód of my réfuge.
58:12 God showeth me his góodness plénteously; * and God shall let me see my désire upon mine énemies.
58:12 Slay them not, lest my péople forgét it; * but scatter them abroad among the people, and put them down, O Lórd our defénse.
58:13 For the sin of their mouth, and for the words of their lips, they shall be táken in their príde: * and why? their talk is of cúrsing and líes.
58:14 Consume them in thy wrath, consume them, that théy may pérish; * and know that it is God that ruleth in Jacob, and unto the énds of the wórld.
58:15 And in the évening they will retúrn, * grin like a dog, and will gó about the cíty.
58:16 They will run here and thére for méat, * and grudge if they bé not sátisfied.
58:17 As for me, I will sing of thy power, and will praise thy mercy betímes in the mórning; * for thou hast been my defense and refuge in the dáy of my tróuble.
58:18 Unto thee, O my stréngth, will I síng; * for thou, O God, art my refuge, and my mérciful Gód.
