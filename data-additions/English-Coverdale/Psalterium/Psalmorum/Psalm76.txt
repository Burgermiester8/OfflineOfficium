76:2 I WILL cry unto Gód with my vóice; * even unto God will I cry with my voice, and he shall hárken unto mé.
76:3 In the time of my trouble I sóught the Lórd: * I stretched forth my hands unto him, and ceased not in the night season; my sóul refused cómfort.
76:4 When I am in heaviness, I will thínk upon Gód; * when my heart is vexed, I wíll compláin.
76:5 Thou hóldest mine eyes wáking: * I am so feeble that I cánnot spéak.
76:6 I have considered the dáys of óld, * and the yéars that are pást.
76:7 I call to remémbrance my sóng, * and in the night I commune with mine own heart, and séarch out my spírit.
76:8 Will the Lord absent himsélf foréver? * and will he be no móre entréated?
76:9 Is his mercy clean góne foréver? * and is his promise come utterly to an énd forévermore?
76:10 Hath God forgótten to be grácious? * and will he shut up his loving-kíndness in displéasure?
76:11 And I said, It is mine ówn infírmity; * but I will remember the years of the right hánd of the Most Híghest.
76:12 I will remember the wórks of the LÓRD, * and call to mind thy wónders of old tíme.
76:13 I will think also of áll thy wórks, * and my talking shall bé of thy dóings.
76:14 Thy way, O Gód, is hóly: * who is so great a Gód as our Gód?
76:15a Thou art the God that dóest wónders, * and hast declared thy power amóng the péoples.
76:15b Thou hast mightily delivered thy people, * even the sons of Jacob and Joseph.
76:17 The waters saw thee, O God, the waters saw thee, and wére afráid; * the depths álso were tróubled.
76:18 The clouds poured out wáter, the air thúndered, * and thine árrows went abróad.
76:19 The voice of thy thunder was heard róund abóut: * the lightnings shone upon the ground; the earth was moved, and shóok withál.
76:20 Thy way is in the sea, and thy páths in the great wáters, * and thy fóotsteps are not knówn.
76:21 Thou leddest thy péople like shéep, * by the hand of Móses and Áaron.
