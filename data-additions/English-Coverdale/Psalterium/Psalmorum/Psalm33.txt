33:2 I WILL alway give thánks unto the LÓRD; * his praise shall ever be ín my móuth.
33:3 My soul shall make her bóast in the LÓRD; * the humble shall héar thereof, and be glád.
33:4 O praise the LÓRD with mé, * and let us magnify his Náme togéther.
33:5 I sought the LÓRD, and he héard me; * yea, he delivered me out of áll my féar.
33:6 They had an eye unto hím, and were líghtened; * and their faces were nót ashámed.
33:7 Lo, the poor crieth, and the LORD héareth hím; * yea, and saveth him out of áll his tróubles.
33:8 The angel of the LORD tarrieth round about thém that féar him, * and delívereth thém.
33:9 O taste, and see, how grácious the LÓRD is: * blessed is the man that trústeth in hím.
33:10 O fear the LORD, ye that áre his sáints; * for they that féar him lack nóthing.
33:11 The lions do lack, and súffer húnger; * but they who seek the LORD shall want no manner of thíng that is góod.
33:12 Come, ye children, and hárken unto mé; * I will teach you the féar of the LÓRD.
33:13 What man is he that lústeth to líve, * and would fáin see good dáys?
33:14 Keep thy tóngue from évil, * and thy lips, that they spéak no guíle.
33:15 Eschew evil, and dó góod; * seek péace, and ensúe it.
33:16 The eyes of the LORD are óver the ríghteous, * and his ears are open únto their práyers.
33:17 The countenánce of the LORD is against thém that do évil, * to root out the remembrance of thém from the éarth.
33:18 The righteous cry, and the LORD héareth thém, * and delivereth them out of áll their tróubles.
33:19 The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a cóntrite héart, * and will save such as be of an húmble spírit.
33:20 Great are the tróubles of the ríghteous; * but the LORD delivereth him óut of áll.
33:21 He keepeth áll his bónes, * so that not one of thém is bróken.
33:22 But misfortune shall sláy the ungódly; * and they that hate the ríghteous shall be désolate.
33:23 The LORD delivereth the sóuls of his sérvants; * and all they that put their trust in him shall nót be déstitute.
