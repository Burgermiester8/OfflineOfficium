103:1 PRAISE the LORD, O my soul: * O LORD my God, thou art become exceeding glorious; thou art clothed with majesty and honor.
103:2 Thou deckest thyself with light as it were with a garment, * and spreadest out the heavens like a curtain.
103:3 Who layeth the beams of his chambers in the waters, * and maketh the clouds his chariot, and walketh upon the wings of the wind.
103:4 He maketh his angels winds, * and his ministers a flaming fire.
103:5 He laid the foundations of the earth, * that it never should move at any time.
103:6 Thou coveredst it with the deep like as with a garment; * the waters stand above the hills.
103:7 At thy rebuke they flee; * at the voice of thy thunder they haste away.
103:8 They go up as high as the hills, and down to the valleys beneath; * even unto the place which thou hast appointed for them.
103:9 Thou hast set them their bounds, which they shall not pass, * neither turn again to cover the earth.
103:10 He sendeth the springs into the rivers, * which run among the hills.
103:11 All beasts of the field drink thereof, * and the wild asses quench their thirst.
103:12 Beside them shall the fowls of the air have their habitation, * and sing among the branches.
103:13 He watereth the hills from above; * the earth is filled with the fruit of thy works.
103:14 He bringeth forth grass for the cattle, * and green herb for the service of men;
103:14 That he may bring food out of the earth, and wine that maketh glad the heart of man; * and oil to make him a cheerful countenance, and bread to strengthen man’s heart.
103:16a The trees of the LORD also are full of sap; * even the cedars of Lebanon which he hath planted;
103:16b Wherein the birds make their nests; * and the fir trees are a dwelling for the stork.
103:17 The high hills are a refuge for the wild goats; * and so are the stony rocks for the conies.
103:19 He appointed the moon for certain seasons, * and the sun knoweth his going down.
103:20 Thou makest darkness that it may be night; * wherein all the beasts of the forest do move.
103:21 The lions, roaring after their prey, * do seek their meat from God.
103:22 The sun ariseth, and they get them away together, * and lay them down in their dens.
103:23 Man goeth forth to his work, and to his labor, * until the evening.
103:24 O LORD, how manifold are thy works! * in wisdom hast thou made them all; the earth is full of thy riches.
103:25 So is the great and wide sea also; * wherein are things creeping innumerable, both small and great beasts.
103:26 There go the ships, and there is that leviathan, * whom thou hast made to take his pastime therein.
103:27 These wait all upon thee, * that thou mayest give them meat in due season.
103:28 When thou givest it them, they gather it; * and when thou openest thy hand, they are filled with good.
103:29 When thou hidest thy face, they are troubled: * when thou takest away their breath, they die, and are turned again to their dust.
103:30 When thou lettest thy breath go forth, they shall be made; * and thou shalt renew the face of the earth.
103:31 The glorious majesty of the LORD shall endure forever; * the LORD shall rejoice in his works.
103:32 The earth shall tremble at the look of him; * if he do but touch the hills, they shall smoke.
103:33 I will sing unto the LORD as long as I live; * I will praise my God while I have my being.
103:34 And so shall my words please him: * my joy shall be in the LORD.
103:35a As for sinners, they shall be consumed out of the earth, * and the ungodly shall come to an end.
103:35b Praise thou the LORD, O my soul. * Praise the LORD.
