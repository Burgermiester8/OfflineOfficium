139:2 DELIVER me, O LORD, from the évil mán; * and preserve me from the wícked mán;
139:3 Who imagine míschief in their héarts, * and stir up strífe all the day lóng.
139:4 They have sharpened their tóngues like a sérpent; * adder’s poison is únder their líps.
139:5 Keep me, O LORD, from the hánds of the ungódly; * preserve me from the wicked men, who are purposed to overthrów my góings.
139:6 The proud have laid a snare for me, and spread a net abróad with córds; * yea, and set tráps in my wáy.
139:7 I said unto the LORD, Thóu art my Gód, * hear the voice of my práyers, O LÓRD.
139:8 O LORD God, thou stréngth of my héalth; * thou hast covered my head in the dáy of báttle.
139:9 Let not the ungodly have his desíre, O LÓRD; * let not his mischievous imagination prosper, † lest they bé too próud.
139:10 Let the mischief of their own lips fall upon the héad of thém * that cómpass me abóut.
139:11 Let hot burning coals fáll upón them; * let them be cast into the fire, † and into the pit, that they never rise úp agáin.
139:12 A man full of words shall not prosper upón the éarth: * evil shall hunt the wicked pérson to overthrów him.
139:13 Sure I am that the LORD will avénge the póor, * and maintain the cáuse of the hélpless.
139:14 The righteous also shall give thánks unto thy Náme; * and the just shall contínue in thy síght.
