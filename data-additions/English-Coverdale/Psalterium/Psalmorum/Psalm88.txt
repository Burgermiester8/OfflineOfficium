88:2 MY song shall be alway of the loving-kindness of the LORD; * with my mouth will I ever be showing thy truth from one generation to another.
88:3 For I have said, Mercy shall be set up forever; * thy truth shalt thou stablish in the heavens.
88:4 I have made a covenant with my chosen; * I have sworn unto David my servant:
88:5 Thy seed will I stablish forever, * and set up thy throne from one generation to another.
88:6 O LORD, the very heavens shall praise thy wondrous works; * and thy truth in the congregation of the saints.
88:7a For who is he among the clouds, * that shall be compared unto the LORD?
88:7b And what is he among the gods, * that shall be like unto the LORD?
88:8 God is very greatly to be feared in the council of the saints, * and to be had in reverence of all them that are round about him.
88:9 O Lord God of hosts, who is like unto thee? * thy truth, most mighty LORD, is on every side.
88:10 Thou rulest the raging of the sea; * thou stillest the waves thereof when they arise.
88:11 Thou hast subdued Egypt, and destroyed it; * thou hast scattered thine enemies abroad with thy mighty arm.
88:12 The heavens are thine, the earth also is thine; * thou hast laid the foundation of the round world, and all that therein is.
88:13 Thou hast made the north and the south; * Tabor and Hermon shall rejoice in thy Name.
88:14 Thou hast a mighty arm; * strong is thy hand, and high is thy right hand.
88:15 Righteousness and equity are the habitation of thy seat; * mercy and truth shall go before thy face.
88:16a Blessed is the people, O LORD, that can rejoice in thee; * they shall walk in the light of thy countenance.
88:16b Their delight shall be daily in thy Name; * and in thy righteousness shall they make their boast.
88:18 For thou art the glory of their strength, * and in thy loving-kindness thou shalt lift up our horns.
88:19 For the LORD is our defense; * the Holy One of Israel is our King.
88:20 THOU spakest sometime in visions unto thy saints, and saidst, * I have laid help upon one that is mighty, I have exalted one chosen out of the people.
88:21 I have found David my servant; * with my holy oil have I anointed him.
88:22 My hand shall hold him fast, * and my arm shall strengthen him.
88:23 The enemy shall not be able to do him violence; * the son of wickedness shall not hurt him.
88:24 I will smite down his foes before his face, * and plague them that hate him.
88:25 My truth also and my mercy shall be with him; * and in my Name shall his horn be exalted.
88:26 I will set his dominion also in the sea, * and his right hand in the floods.
88:27 He shall call me, Thou art my Father, * my God, and my strong salvation.
88:28 And I will make him my firstborn, * higher than the kings of the earth.
88:29 My mercy will I keep for him forevermore, * and my covenant shall stand fast with him.
88:30 His seed also will I make to endure forever, * and his throne as the days of heaven.
88:31 But if his children forsake my law, * and walk not in my judgments;
88:32 If they break my statutes, and keep not my commandments; * I will visit their offences with the rod, and their sin with scourges.
88:34 Nevertheless, my loving-kindness will I not utterly take from him, * nor suffer my truth to fail.
88:35 My covenant will I not break, nor alter the thing that is gone out of my lips: * I have sworn once by my holiness, that I will not fail David.
88:37a His seed shall endure forever, * and his throne is like as the sun before me.
88:37b He shall stand fast forevermore as the moon, * and as the faithful witness in heaven.
88:39 But thou hast abhorred and forsaken thine anointed, * and art displeased at him.
88:40 Thou hast broken the covenant of thy servant, * and cast his crown to the ground.
88:41 Thou hast overthrown all his hedges, * and broken down his strongholds.
88:42 All they that go by spoil him, * and he is become a reproach to his neighbors.
88:43 Thou hast set up the right hand of his enemies, * and made all his adversaries to rejoice.
88:44 Thou hast taken away the edge of his sword, * and givest him not victory in the battle.
88:45 Thou hast put out his glory, * and cast his throne down to the ground.
88:46 The days of his youth hast thou shortened, * and covered him with dishonor.
88:47 LORD, how long wilt thou hide thyself? forever? * and shall thy wrath burn like fire?
88:48 O remember how short my time is; * wherefore hast thou made all men for nought?
88:49 What man is he that liveth, and shall not see death? * and shall he deliver his soul from the power of the grave?
88:50 Lord, where are thy old loving-kindnesses, * which thou swarest unto David in thy truth?
88:51 Remember, Lord, the rebuke that thy servants have, * and how I do bear in my bosom the rebukes of many people;
88:52 Wherewith thine enemies have blasphemed thee, * and slandered the footsteps of thine anointed.
88:53 Praised be the LORD forevermore. * Amen, and Amen.
