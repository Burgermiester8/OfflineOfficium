64:2 THOU, O God, art práised in Síon; * and unto thee shall the vow be perfórmed in Jerúsalem.
64:3 Thou that héarest the práyer, * unto thee shall áll flesh cóme.
64:4 My misdeeds preváil agáinst me: * O be thou mérciful unto our síns.
64:5 Blessed is the man whom thou choosest, and recéivest unto thée: * he shall dwell in thy court, and shall be satisfied with the pleasures of thy house, even of thy hóly témple.
64:6 Thou shalt show us wonderful things in thy righteousness, O Gód of our salvátion; * thou that art the hope of all the ends of the earth, and of them that remáin in the broad séa.
64:7a Who in his strength setteth fást the móuntains, * and is girded abóut with pówer.
64:7b Who stilleth the ráging of the séa, * and the noise of his waves, and the mádness of the péoples.
64:8 They also that dwell in the uttermost parts of the earth shall be afráid at thy tókens, * thou that makest the outgoings of the morning and évening to práise thee.
64:10 Thou visitest the éarth, and bléssest it; * thou makest it véry plénteous.
64:10 The river of God is fúll of wáter: * thou preparest their corn, for so thou provídest for the éarth.
64:11 Thou waterest her furrows; thou sendest rain into the little válleys theréof; * thou makest it soft with the drops of rain, and blessest the íncrease óf it.
64:12 Thou crownest the yéar with thy góodness; * and thy clóuds drop fátness.
64:13 They shall drop upon the dwéllings of the wílderness; * and the little hills shall rejoice on évery síde.
64:14 The folds shall be fúll of shéep; * the valleys also shall stand so thick with corn, that they shall láugh and síng.
