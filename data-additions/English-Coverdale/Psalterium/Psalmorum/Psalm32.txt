32:1 REJOICE in the LÓRD, O ye ríghteous; * for it becometh well the júst to be thánkful.
32:2 Praise the LÓRD with hárp; * sing praises unto him with the lute, and ínstrument of tén strings.
32:3 Sing únto the Lórd a new sóng; * sing práises lústily unto hím with a good cóurage.
32:4 For the word of the LÓRD is trúe; * and all his wórks are fáithful.
32:5 He loveth ríghteousness and júdgment; * the earth is full of the góodness of the LÓRD.
32:6 By the word of the LORD were the héavens máde; * and all the host of them by the bréath of his móuth.
32:7 He gathereth the waters of the sea together, as it were upón an héap; * and layeth up the deep, as in a tréasure-hóuse.
32:8 Let all the earth féar the LÓRD: * stand in awe of him, all ye that dwéll in the wórld.
32:9 For he spake, and ít was dóne; * he commanded, and ít stood fást.
32:10 The LORD bringeth the counsel of the héathen to nóught, * and maketh the devices of the people to be of none effect, and casteth out the cóunsels of prínces.
32:11 The counsel of the LORD shall endúre foréver, * and the thoughts of his heart from generátion to generátion.
32:12 Blessed are the people whose God is the Lórd JEHÓVAH; * and blessed are the folk that he hath chosen to him, to bé his inhéritance.
32:13 The LORD looketh down from heaven, and beholdeth all the chíldren of mén; * from the habitation of his dwelling, he considereth all them that dwéll on the éarth.
32:15 He fashioneth all the heárts of thém, * and understandeth áll their wórks.
32:16 There is no king that can be saved by the múltitude of an hóst; * neither is any mighty man delívered by much stréngth.
32:17 A horse is counted but a vain thing to sáve a mán; * neither shall he deliver any mán by his great stréngth.
32:18 Behold, the eye of the LORD is upón them that féar him, * and upon them that put their trúst in his mércy;
32:19 To deliver their sóul from déath, * and to feed them in the tíme of déarth.
32:20 Our soul hath patiently tárried for the LÓRD; * for he is our hélp and our shíeld.
32:21 For our heart shall rejóice in hím; * because we have hoped in his hóly Náme.
32:22 Let thy merciful kindness, O LÓRD, be upón us, * like as we do put our trúst in theé.
