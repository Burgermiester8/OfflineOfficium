11:2 HELP me, LORD, for there is not one gódly man léft; * for the faithful are minished from among the chíldren of mén.
11:3 They talk of vanity every óne with his néighbor; * they do but flatter with their lips, and dissemble in théir double héart.
11:4 The LORD shall root out all decéitful líps, * and the tongue that spéaketh proud thíngs;
11:5 Which have said, With our tóngue will we preváil; * we are they that ought to speak; who is lórd over ús?
11:6a Now, for the comfortless troubles’ sáke of the néedy, * and because of the deep síghing of the póor,
11:6b I will up, sáith the LÓRD; * and will help every one from him that swelleth against him, and will sét him at rést.
11:7 The words of the LÓRD are pure wórds; * even as the silver which from the earth is tried, and purified seven tímes in the fíre.
11:8 Thou shalt keep thém, O LÓRD; * thou shalt preserve them from this generátion foréver.
11:9 The ungodly walk on évery síde: * when they are exalted, the children of men are pút to rebúke.
