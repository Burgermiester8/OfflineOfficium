120:1 I WILL lift up mine eyes únto the hílls; * from whence cómeth my hélp?
120:2 My help cometh éven from the Lórd, * who hath made heáven and eárth.
120:3 He will not suffer thy foót to be móved; * and he that kéepeth thee will nót sleep.
120:4 Behold, he that kéepeth Ísrael * shall neither slúmber nor sléep.
120:5 The Lord himsélf is thy kéeper; * the Lord is thy defense upon thy ríght hánd;
120:6 So that the sun shall not búrn thee by dáy, * neither the moón by níght.
120:7 The Lord shall presérve thee from all évil; * yea, it is even he that shall keép thy soúl.
120:8 The Lord shall preserve thy going out, and thy cóming ín, * from this time forth forévermóre.
