126:1a EXCEPT the Lord buíld the hoúse, * their labor is but lóst that buíld it.
126:1b Except the Lord kéep the cíty, * the watchman wáketh but in vaín.
126:2a It is but lost labor that ye haste to rise up early, and so late take rest, and eat the bréad of cárefulness; * for so he giveth his belóved sléep.
126:2b Lo, children, and the fruít of the wómb, * are an heritage and gift that cómeth of the Lórd.
126:4 Like as the arrows in the hánd of the gíant, * even so are the yóung chíldren.
126:5 Happy is the man that hath his quíver fúll of them; * they shall not be ashamed when they speak with their énemies in the gáte.
