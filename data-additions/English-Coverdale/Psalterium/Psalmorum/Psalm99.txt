99:2 O BE joyful in the LORD, all ye lands: * serve the LORD with gladness, and come before his presence with a song.
99:3 Be ye sure that the LORD he is God; it is he that hath made us, and not we ourselves; * we are his people, and the sheep of his pasture.
99:4b O go your way into his gates with thanksgiving, and into his courts with praise; * be thankful unto him, and speak good of his Name.
99:5 For the LORD is gracious, his mercy is everlasting; * and his truth endureth from generation to generation.
