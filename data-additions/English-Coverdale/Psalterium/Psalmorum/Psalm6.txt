6:2 LORD, rebuke me not in thíne indignàtion, * neither chasten me in thý displéasure.
6:3 Have mercy upon me, O LORD, for Í am wéak; * O LORD, heal me, for my bónes are véxed.
6:4 My soul also ís sore tróubled: * but, LORD, how lóng wilt thou púnish me?
6:5 Turn thee, O LORD, and delíver my sóul; * O save me, for thy mércy’s sáke.
6:6 For in death no man remémbereth thée; * and who will give thee thánks in the pít?
6:7 I am wéary of my gróaning; * every night wash I my bed, and water my cóuch with my téars.
6:8 My beauty is gone for véry tróuble, * and worn away because of áll mine énemies.
6:9 Away from me, all ye that wórk iníquity; * for the LORD hath heard the vóice of my wéeping.
6:10 The LORD hath héard my petítion; * the LORD will recéive my práyer.
6:11 All mine enemies shall be confóunded, and sore véxed; * they shall be turned back, and pút to shame súddenly.
