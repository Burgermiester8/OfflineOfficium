89:1 LORD, thou hast been our refuge, * from one generation to another.
89:2 Before the mountains were brought forth, or ever the earth and the world were made, * thou art God from everlasting, and world without end.
89:3 Thou turnest man to destruction; * again thou sayest, Come again, ye children of men.
89:4 For a thousand years in thy sight are but as yesterday when it is past, * and as a watch in the night.
89:6a As soon as thou scatterest them they are even as a sleep; * and fade away suddenly like the grass.
89:6b In the morning it is green, and groweth up; * but in the evening it is cut down, dried up, and withered.
89:7 For we consume away in thy displeasure, * and are afraid at thy wrathful indignation.
89:8 Thou hast set our misdeeds before thee; * and our secret sins in the light of thy countenance.
89:9 For when thou art angry all our days are gone: * we bring our years to an end, as it were a tale that is told.
89:10 The days of our age are threescore years and ten; and though men be so strong that they come to fourscore years, * yet is their strength then but labor and sorrow; so soon passeth it away, and we are gone.
89:11 But who regardeth the power of thy wrath? * or feareth aright thy indignation?
89:12 So teach us to number our days, * that we may apply our hearts unto wisdom.
89:13 Turn thee again, O LORD, at the last, * and be gracious unto thy servants.
89:14 O satisfy us with thy mercy, and that soon: * so shall we rejoice and be glad all the days of our life.
89:15 Comfort us again now after the time that thou hast plagued us; * and for the years wherein we have suffered adversity.
89:16 Show thy servants thy work, * and their children thy glory.
89:17 And the glorious majesty of the LORD our God be upon us: * prosper thou the work of our hands upon us; O prosper thou our handy-work.
