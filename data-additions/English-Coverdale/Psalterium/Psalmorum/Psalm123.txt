123:1 IF the Lord himself had not been on our side, now may Ísrael sáy; * if the Lord himself had not been on our side, when men rose úp agaínst us;
123:2b They had swallowed us úp alíve; * when they were so wrathfully displeásed at ús.
123:3b Yea, the wáters had drówned us, * and the stream had gone óver our soúl.
123:5 The deep wáters of the proúd * had gone even óver our soúl.
123:6 But praísed be the Lórd, * who hath not given us over for a prey únto their teéth.
123:7 Our soul is escaped even as a bird out of the snáre of the fówler; * the snare is broken, and wé are delívered.
123:8 Our help standeth in the Náme of the Lórd, * who hath made heáven and eárth.
