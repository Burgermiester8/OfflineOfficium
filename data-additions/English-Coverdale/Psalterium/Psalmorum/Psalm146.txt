146:1 PRAISE the LORD, for it is a good thing to sing praises únto our Gód; * yea, a joyful and pleasant thing it ís to be thánkful.
146:2 The LORD doth búild up Jerúsalem, * and gather together the óutcasts of Ísrael.
146:3 He healeth those that are bróken in héart, * and giveth medicine to héal their síckness.
146:4 He telleth the númber of the stárs, * and calleth them all bý their námes.
146:5 Great is our Lord, and gréat is his pówer; * yea, and his wísdom is ínfinite.
146:6 The LORD sétteth up the méek, * and bringeth the ungodly dówn to the gróund.
146:7 O sing unto the LÓRD with thanksgíving; * sing praises upon the harp únto our Gód:
146:8 Who covereth the heaven with clouds, and prepareth ráin for the éarth; * and maketh the grass to grow upon the mountains, † and herb for the úse of mén;
146:9 Who giveth fodder únto the cáttle, * and feedeth the young ravens that cáll upón him.
146:10 He hath no pleasure in the stréngth of an hórse; * neither delighteth he in ány man’s légs.
146:11 But the LORD’s delight is in thém that fear hím, * and put their trúst in his mércy.
