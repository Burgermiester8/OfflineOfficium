7:2 O LORD my God, in thee have I pút my trúst: * save me from all them that pérsecute me, and déliver me;
7:3 Lest he devour my soul like a lion, and téar it in píeces, * while there is nóne to hélp.
7:4 O LORD my God, if I have done ány such thíng; * or if there be any wíckedness in my hánds;
7:5 If I have rewarded evil unto him that dealt fríendly with mé; * yea, I have delivered him that without any cáuse is mine énemy;
7:6 Then let mine enemy persecute my sóul, and táke me; * yea, let him tread my life down upon the earth, and lay mine hónor in the dúst.
7:7 Stand up, O LORD, in thy wrath, and lift up thyself, because of the indignátion of mine énemies; * arise up for me in the judgment that hast thou commànded.
7:8b And so shall the congregation of the péoples come abóut thee: * for their sakes therefore lift up thysélf agáin.
7:9b The LORD shall judge the peoples: give séntence with me, O LÓRD, * according to my righteousness, and according to the ínnocency that is ín me.
7:10a O let the wickedness of the ungodly cóme to an énd; * but guide thóu the júst.
7:10b For the ríghteous Gód * trieth the very héarts and réins.
7:11 My help cómeth of Gód, * who preserveth them that are trúe of héart.
7:12 God is a righteous Judge, stróng, and pátient; * and God is provóked every dáy.
7:13 If a man will not turn, he will whét his swórd; * he hath bent his bow, and máde it réady.
7:14 He hath prepared for him the ínstruments of déath; * he ordaineth his árrows against the pérsecutors.
7:15 Behold, the ungodly traváileth with iníquity; * he hath conceived mischief, and bróught forth fálsehood.
7:16 He hath graven and dígged up a pít, * and is fallen himself into the destruction that he máde for óther.
7:17 For his travail shall come upón his own héad, * and his wickedness shall fáll on his own páte.
7:18 I will give thanks unto the LORD, accórding to his ríghteousness; * and I will praise the Name of the LÓRD Most Hígh.
