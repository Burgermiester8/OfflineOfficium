28:1 ASCRIBE unto the LORD, Ó ye míghty, * ascribe unto the LORD wórship and stréngth.
28:2 Ascribe unto the LORD the honor due untó his Náme; * worship the LORD with hóly wórship.
28:3 The voice of the LORD is upón the wáters; * it is the glorious God that máketh the thúnder.
28:4 It is the LORD that ruleth the sea; † the voice of the LORD is míghty in operátion; * the voice of the LORD is a glórious vóice.
28:5 The voice of the LORD breaketh the cédar trées; * yea, the LORD breaketh the cédars of Lébanon.
28:6 He maketh them also to skíp like a cálf; * Lebanon also, and Sirion, líke a young únicorn.
28:7 The voice of the LORD divideth the flames of fire; † the voice of the LORD sháketh the wílderness; * yea, the LORD shaketh the wílderness of Kádesh.
28:9 The voice of the LORD maketh the hinds to bring forth young, and strippeth báre the fórests: * in his temple doth every thing spéak of his hónor.
28:10 The LORD sitteth abóve the wáterflood, * and the LORD remaineth a Kíng foréver.
28:11 The LORD shall give stréngth unto his péople; * the LORD shall give his people the bléssing of péace.
