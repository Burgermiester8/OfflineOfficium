83:2 O HOW amiable are thy dwellings, * thou LORD of hosts.
83:3 My soul hath a desire and longing to enter into the courts of the LORD; * my heart and my flesh rejoice in the living God.
83:4 Yea, the sparrow hath found her an house, and the swallow a nest, where she may lay her young; * even thy altars, O LORD of hosts, my King and my God.
83:5 Blessed are they that dwell in thy house; * they will be alway praising thee.
83:6a Blessed is the man whose strength is in thee; * in whose heart are thy ways.
83:6b Who going through the vale of misery use it for a well; * and the pools are filled with water.
83:8 They will go from strength to strength, * and unto the God of gods appeareth every one of them in Sion.
83:9 O LORD God of hosts, hear my prayer; * harken, O God of Jacob.
83:10 Behold, O God our defender, * and look upon the face of thine anointed.
83:11 For one day in thy courts * is better than a thousand.
83:11 I had rather be a doorkeeper in the house of my God, * than to dwell in the tents of ungodliness.
83:12 For the LORD God is a light and defense; * the LORD will give grace and worship; and no good thing shall he withhold from them that live a godly life.
83:13 O LORD God of hosts, * blessed is the man that putteth his trust in thee.
