73:1 O GOD, wherefore art thou absent from ús so lóng? * why is thy wrath so hot against the shéep of thy pásture?
73:2 O think upon thy cóngregátion, * whom thou hast purchased, and redéemed of óld.
73:2 Think upon the tribe of thíne inhéritance, * and Mount Sion, wherein thóu hast dwélt.
73:3 Lift up thy feet, that thou mayest utterly destroy évery énemy, * which hath done évil in thy sánctuary.
73:4 Thine adversaries roar in the midst of thy cóngregátions, * and set up their bánners for tókens.
73:5 He that hewed timber afore óut of the thick trées, * was known to bring it to an éxcellent wórk.
73:6 But now they break down all the carved wórk thereóf * with áxes and hámmers.
73:7 They have set fire upon thy hóly pláces, * and have defiled the dwelling-place of thy Name, even únto the gróund.
73:8 Yea, they said in their hearts, Let us make havoc of them áltogéther: * thus have they burnt up all the houses of Gód in the lánd.
73:9 We see not our tokens; there is not one próphet móre; * no, not one is there among us, that understandeth ány móre.
73:10 O God, how long shall the adversary dó this dishónor? * shall the enemy blaspheme thy Náme foréver?
73:11 Why withdrawest thóu thy hánd? * why pluckest thou not thy right hand out of thy bosom to consúme the énemy?
73:12 For God is my Kíng of óld; * the help that is done upon earth, he dóeth it himsélf.
73:13 Thou didst divide the sea thróugh thy pówer; * thou brakest the heads of the drágons in the wáters.
73:14 Thou smotest the heads of levíathan in píeces, * and gavest him to be meat for the péople of the wílderness.
73:15 Thou broughtest out fountains and waters óut of the hard rócks; * thou driedst up míghty wáters.
73:16 The day is thine, and the níght is thíne; * thou hast prepared the líght and the sún.
73:17 Thou hast set all the borders óf the éarth; * thou hast made súmmer and wínter.
73:18 Remember this, O LORD, how the énemy hath rebúked; * and how the foolish people hath blásphemed thy Náme.
73:19 O deliver not the soul of thy turtledove unto the múltitude of the énemies; * and forget not the congregation of the póor foréver.
73:20 Look upón the cóvenant; * for all the earth is full of darkness and crúel habitátions.
73:21 O let not the simple go awáy ashámed; * but let the poor and needy give praise únto thy Náme.
73:22 Arise, O God, maintáin thine own cáuse; * remember how the foolish man blasphémeth thee dáily.
73:23 Forget not the vóice of thine énemies: * the presumption of them that hate thee increaseth ever móre and móre.
