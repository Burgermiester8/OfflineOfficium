136:1 BY the waters of Babylon we sát down and wépt, * when we remembered thée, O Síon.
136:2 As for our harps, we hánged them úp * upon the trees that áre thereín.
136:3 For they that led us away captive, † required of us then a song, and mélody in our héaviness: * Sing us one of the sóngs of Síon.
136:4 How shall we síng the LORD’s sóng * ín a strange lánd?
136:5 If I forgét thee, O Jerúsalem, * let my right hand forgét her cúnning.
136:6 If I do not remember thee, † let my tongue cleave to the róof of my móuth; * yea, if I prefer not Jerusalem abóve my chief jóy.
136:7 Remember the children of Edom, O LORD, in the dáy of Jerúsalem; * how they said, Down with it, down with it, éven to the gróund.
136:8 O daughter of Babylon, wásted with mísery; * yea, happy shall he be that rewardeth thee as thóu hast sérved us.
136:9 Blessed shall he be that táketh thy chíldren, * and throweth them agáinst the stónes.
