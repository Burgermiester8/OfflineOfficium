61:2 MY soul truly waiteth stíll upon Gód; * for of him cómeth my salvátion.
61:3 He verily is my stréngth and my salvátion; * he is my defense, so that I shall nót greatly fáll.
61:4 How long will ye imagine mischief against évery mán? * Ye shall be slain all the sort of you; yea, as a tottering wall shall ye be, and like a bróken hédge.
61:5 Their device is only how to put him out whom Gód will exált; * their delight is in lies; they give good words with their mouth, but cúrse with their héart.
61:6 Nevertheless, my soul, wait thou stíll upon Gód; * for my hópe is in hím.
61:7 He truly is my stréngth and my salvátion; * he is my defense, so that I sháll not fáll.
61:8 In God is my héalth and my glóry; * the rock of my might; and in Gód is my trúst.
61:9 O put your trust in him álway, ye péople; * pour out your hearts before him, for Gód is our hópe.
61:10 As for the children of men, they are but vanity; the children of mén are decéitful; * upon the weights they are altogether lighter than vánity itsélf.
61:11 O trust not in wrong and robbery; give not yoursélves unto vánity: * if riches increase, set not your héart upón them.
61:12a God spake once, and twice I have also héard the sáme, * that power belóngeth unto Gód;
61:12b And that thou, Lord, árt mérciful; * for thou rewardest every man accórding to his wórk.
