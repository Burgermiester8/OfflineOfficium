128:1 MANY a time have they fought against me from my yóuth úp, * may Ísrael now sáy;
128:2 Yea, many a time have they vexed me from my yóuth úp; * but they have not preváiled agáinst me.
128:3 The plowers plowed upón my báck, * and máde long fúrrows.
128:4 But the ríghteous Lórd * hath hewn the snares of the ungódly in piéces.
128:5 Let them be confounded and túrned báckward, * as many as have evil wíll at Síon.
128:6 Let them be even as the grass upón the hoúsetops, * which withereth afore it be grówn úp;
128:7 Whereof the mower filleth nót his hánd, * neither he that bindeth up the shéaves his bósom.
128:8 So that they who go by say not so much as, The Lord prósper yóu; * we wish you good luck in the Náme of the Lórd.
