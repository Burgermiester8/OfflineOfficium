53:3 SAVE me, O God, for thy Name’s sake, * and avenge me in thy strength.
53:4 Hear my prayer, O God, * and harken unto the words of my mouth.
53:5 For strangers are risen up against me; * and tyrants, which have not God before their eyes, seek after my soul.
53:6 Behold, God is my helper; * the Lord is with them that uphold my soul.
53:7 He shall reward evil unto mine enemies: * destroy thou them in thy truth.
53:8 An offering of a free heart will I give thee, and praise thy Name, O LORD; * because it is so comfortable.
53:9 For he hath delivered me out of all my trouble; * and mine eye hath seen his desire upon mine enemies.
