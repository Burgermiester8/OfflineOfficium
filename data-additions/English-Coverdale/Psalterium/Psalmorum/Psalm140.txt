140:1 LORD, I call upon thee; haste thee únto mé, * and consider my voice, when I crý unto thée.
140:2 Let my prayer be set forth in thy síght as the íncense; * and let the lifting up of my hands be an évening sácrifice.
140:3 Set a watch, O LORD, befóre my móuth, * and keep the dóor of my líps.
140:4 O let not mine heart be inclined to any évil thíng; * let me not be occupied in ungodly works with the men that work wickedness, † neither let me eat of such thíngs as pléase them.
140:5 Let the righteous rather smite me fríendly, and repróve me; * yea, let not my head refuse their précious bálms.
140:6 Ás for the ungódly, * I will pray yet agáinst their wíckedness.
140:7 Let their judges be overthrown in stóny pláces, * that they may hear my words; for théy are swéet.
140:8a Our bones lie scattered befóre the pít, * like as when one breaketh and heweth wóod upon the éarth.
140:8b But mine eyes look unto thée, O LORD Gód; * in thee is my trust; O cast not óut my sóul.
140:9 Keep me from the snare that they have láid for mé, * and from the traps of the wícked dóers.
140:10 Let the ungodly fall into their own néts togéther, * and let me éver escápe them.
