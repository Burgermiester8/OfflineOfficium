12:1 HOW long wilt thou forget me, O LÓRD; foréver? * how long wilt thou hide thy fáce from mé?
12:2 How long shall I seek counsel in my soul, and be so véxed in my héart? * how long shall mine enemy tríumph over mé?
12:3 Consider, and hear me, O LÓRD my Gód; * lighten mine eyes, that I sléep not in déath;
12:4b Lest mine enemy say, I have preváiled against hím: * for if I be cast down, they that trouble me will rejóice at ít.
12:5b But my trust is ín thy mércy, * and my heart is jóyful in thy salvátion.
12:6b I will sing of the LORD, because he hath dealt so lóvingly with mé; * yea, I will praise the Name of the Lórd Most Híghest.
