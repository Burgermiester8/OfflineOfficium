66:2 GOD be merciful unto ús, and bléss us, * and show us the light of his countenance, and be mérciful unto ús;
66:3 That thy way may be knówn upon éarth, * thy saving health amóng all nátions.
66:4 Let the peoples práise thee, O Gód; * yea, let all the péoples práise thee.
66:5 O let the nations rejóice and be glád; * for thou shalt judge the folk righteously, and govern the nátions upon éarth.
66:6 Let the peoples práise thee, O Gód; * yea, let all the péoples práise thee.
66:7a Then shall the earth bring fórth her íncrease; * and God, even our own God, shall gíve us his bléssing.
66:7b Gód shall bléss us; * and all the ends of the wórld shall féar him.
