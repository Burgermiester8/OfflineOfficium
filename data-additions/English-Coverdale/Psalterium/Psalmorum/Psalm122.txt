122:1 UNTO thee lift I úp mine éyes, * O thou that dwéllest in the heávens.
122:2 Behold, even as the eyes of servants look unto the hand of their masters, † and as the eyes of a maiden unto the hánd of her místress, * even so our eyes wait upon the Lord our God, until he have mércy upón us.
122:3 Have mercy upon us, O Lord, have mércy upon ús; * for we are útterly despísed.
122:4 Our soul is filled with the scornful reproóf of the weálthy, * and with the despítefulness of the proúd.
