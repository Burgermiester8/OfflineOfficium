13:2 The LORD looked down from heaven upon the chíldren of mén, * to see if there were any that would understand, and séek after Gód.
13:3a But they are all gone out of the way, they are altogether becóme abóminable; * there is none that doeth good, nó not óne.
13:3d Have they no knowledge, that they are all such wórkers of míschief, * eating up my people as it were bread, and call not upón the LÓRD ?
13:5 There were they brought in great fear, even where nó fear wás; * for God is in the generátion of the ríghteous.
13:6 As for you, ye have made a mock at the cóunsel of the póor; * because he putteth his trúst in the LÓRD.
13:7 Who shall give salvation unto Ísrael out of Síon? * When the LORD turneth the captivity of his people, then shall Jacob rejoice, and Ísrael shall be glád.
