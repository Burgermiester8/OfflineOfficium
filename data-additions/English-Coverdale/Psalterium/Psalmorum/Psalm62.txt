62:2a O GOD, thou árt my Gód; * éarly will I séek thee.
62:2b My soul thirsteth for thee, my flesh also lóngeth after thée, * in a barren and dry land where no wáter ís.
62:3 Thus have I looked for thee ín the sánctuary, * that I might behold thy pówer and glóry.
62:4 For thy loving-kindness is better than the lífe itsélf: * my líps shall práise thee.
62:5 As long as I live will I magnify thee ín this mánner, * and lift up my hánds in thy Náme.
62:6 My soul shall be satisfied, even as it were with márrow and fátness, * when my mouth praiseth thee with jóyful líps.
62:7 Have I not remémbered thee in my béd, * and thought upon thee when Í was wáking?
62:8b Because thou hast béen my hélper; * therefore under the shadow of thy wings will Í rejóice.
62:9 My soul hángeth upón thee; * thy right hand háth uphólden me.
62:10 These also that seek the húrt of my sóul, * they shall go únder the éarth.
62:11 Let them fall upon the édge of the swórd, * that they may be a pórtion for fóxes.
62:12 But the King shall rejoice in God; all they also that swear by him shall bé comménded; * for the mouth of them that speak líes shall be stópped.
