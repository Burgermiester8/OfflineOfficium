69:2 HASTE thee, O Gód, to delíver me; * make haste to hélp me, O LÓRD.
69:3 Let them be ashamed and confounded that seek áfter my sóul; * let them be turned backward and put to confusion that wísh me évil.
69:4 Let them for their reward be soon bróught to sháme, * that crý over me, Thére! there!
69:5 But let all those that seek thee be joyful and glád in thée: * and let all such as delight in thy salvation say alway, The Lórd be práised.
69:6 As for me, I am póor and in mísery: * haste thee unto mé, O Gód.
69:6 Thou art my hélper, and my redéemer: * O LORD, máke no long tárrying.
