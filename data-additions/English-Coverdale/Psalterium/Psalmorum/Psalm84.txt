84:2 LORD, thou art become gracious unto thy land; * thou hast turned away the captivity of Jacob.
84:3 Thou hast forgiven the offence of thy people, * and covered all their sins.
84:4 Thou hast taken away all thy displeasure, * and turned thyself from thy wrathful indignation.
84:5 Turn us then, O God our Savior, * and let thine anger cease from us.
84:6 Wilt thou be displeased at us forever? * and wilt thou stretch out thy wrath from one generation to another?
84:7 Wilt thou not turn again, and quicken us, * that thy people may rejoice in thee?
84:8 Show us thy mercy, O LORD, * and grant us thy salvation.
84:9 I will harken what the LORD God will say; * for he shall speak peace unto his people, and to his saints, that they turn not again unto foolishness.
84:10 For his salvation is nigh them that fear him; * that glory may dwell in our land.
84:11 Mercy and truth are met together: * righteousness and peace have kissed each other.
84:12 Truth shall flourish out of the earth, * and righteousness hath looked down from heaven.
84:13 Yea, the LORD shall show loving-kindness; * and our land shall give her increase.
84:14 Righteousness shall go before him, * and shall direct his going in the way.
