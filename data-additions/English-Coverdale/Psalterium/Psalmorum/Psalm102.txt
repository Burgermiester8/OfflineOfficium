102:1 PRAISE the LORD, O my soul; * and all that is within me, praise his holy Name.
102:2 Praise the LORD, O my soul, * and forget not all his benefits:
102:3 Who forgiveth all thy sin, * and healeth all thine infirmities;
102:4 Who saveth thy life from destruction, * and crowneth thee with mercy and loving-kindness;
102:5 Who satisfieth thy mouth with good things, * making thee young and lusty as an eagle.
102:6 The LORD executeth righteousness and judgment * for all them that are oppressed with wrong.
102:7 He showed his ways unto Moses, * his works unto the children of Israel.
102:8 The LORD is full of compassion and mercy, * long-suffering, and of great goodness.
102:9 He will not alway be chiding; * neither keepeth he his anger forever.
102:10 He hath not dealt with us after our sins; * nor rewarded us according to our wickednesses.
102:11 For look how high the heaven is in comparison of the earth; * so great is his mercy also toward them that fear him.
102:12 Look how wide also the east is from the west; * so far hath he set our sins from us.
102:13 Yea, like as a father pitieth his own children; * even so is the LORD merciful unto them that fear him.
102:14a For he knoweth whereof we are made; * he remembereth that we are but dust.
102:14b The days of man are but as grass; * for he flourisheth as a flower of the field.
102:16 For as soon as the wind goeth over it, it is gone; * and the place thereof shall know it no more.
102:17 But the merciful goodness of the LORD endureth for ever and ever upon them that fear him; * and his righteousness upon children’s children;
102:18 Even upon such as keep his covenant, * and think upon his commandments to do them.
102:19 The LORD hath prepared his seat in heaven, * and his kingdom ruleth over all.
102:20 O praise the LORD, ye angels of his, ye that excel in strength; * ye that fulfill his commandment, and harken unto the voice of his word.
102:21 O praise the LORD, all ye his hosts; * ye servants of his that do his pleasure.
102:22 O speak good of the LORD, all ye works of his, in all places of his dominion: * praise thou the LORD, O my soul.
