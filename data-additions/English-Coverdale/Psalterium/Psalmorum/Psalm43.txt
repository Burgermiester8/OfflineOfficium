43:2 WE have heard with our ears, O Gód, our fáthers have tóld us * what thou hast dóne in their tíme of óld:
43:3 How thou hast driven out the heathen with thy hánd, and plánted our fáthers in; * how thou hast destroyed the nations, and máde thy péople to flóurish.
43:4 For they gat not the lánd in posséssion through their ówn sword, * neither was it their ówn arm that hélped thém;
43:4 But thy right hand, and thíne arm, and the líght of thy cóuntenance; * because thou hádst a fávor únto them.
43:5 Thou árt my Kíng, O Gód; * send hélp únto Jácob.
43:6 Through thee will we óverthrów our énemies, * and in thy Name will we tread them únder that ríse up agáinst us.
43:7 For I wíll not trúst in my bów, * it is nót my swórd that shall hélp me;
43:8 But it is thou that sávest ús from our énemies, * and púttest them to confúsion that háte us.
43:9 We make our bóast of Gód all day lóng, * and will práise thy Náme foréver.
43:10 But now thou art fár off, and púttest us to confúsion, * and góest not fórth with our ármies.
43:11 Thou makest us to túrn our bácks upon our énemies, * so that they which háte us spóil our góods.
43:12 Thou lettest us be éaten up líke shéep, * and hast scáttered us amóng the héathen.
43:13 Thou séllest thy péople for nóught, * and tákest no móney for them.
43:14 Thou makest ús to be rebúked of our néighbors, * to be laughed to scorn, and had in derision of thém that are róund abóut us.
43:15 Thou makest us to be a býword amóng the nátions, * and that the péoples sháke their héads at us.
43:16 My confúsion is dáily befóre me, * and the sháme of my fáce hath cóvered me;
43:17 For the vóice of the slánderer and blasphémer, * for the énemy and avénger.
43:18 And though all this be come upon us, yét do we nót forgét thee, * nor behave oursélves frówardly in thy cóvenant.
43:19 Our héart is nót turned báck, * neither our stéps gone óut of thy wáy;
43:20 No, not when thou hast smítten us into the pláce of drágons, * and covered us wíth the shádow of déath.
43:21 If we have forgotten the Name of our God, and hólden up our hánds to any stránge god, * shall not God search it out? for he knóweth the very sécrets of the héart.
43:22 For thy sake álso are we kílled all the dáy long, * and are counted as shéep appóinted to be sláin.
43:23 Úp, Lord, why sléepest thóu? * awake, and be not ábsent from ús foréver.
43:24 Wherefore hídest thóu thy fáce, * and forgéttest our mísery and tróuble?
43:25 For our soul is brought low, éven únto the dúst; * our belly cláaveth únto the gróund.
43:26 Aríse, and hélp us, * and delíver us, for thy mércy’s sáke.
