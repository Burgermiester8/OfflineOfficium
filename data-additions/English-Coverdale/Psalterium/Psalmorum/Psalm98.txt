98:1 THE LORD is King, be the people never so impatient; * he sitteth between the Cherubim, be the earth never so unquiet.
98:2 The LORD is great in Sion, * and high above all people.
98:3 They shall give thanks unto thy Name, * which is great, wonderful, and holy.
98:4 The King’s power loveth judgment; thou hast prepared equity, * thou hast executed judgment and righteousness in Jacob.
98:5 O magnify the LORD our God, and fall down before his footstool; * for he is holy.
98:6 Moses and Aaron among his priests, and Samuel among such as call upon his Name: * these called upon the LORD, and he heard them.
98:7 He spake unto them out of the cloudy pillar; * for they kept his testimonies, and the law that he gave them.
98:8 Thou heardest them, O LORD our God; * thou forgavest them, O God, though thou didst punish their wicked doings.
98:9 O magnify the LORD our God, and worship him upon his holy hill; * for the LORD our God is holy.
