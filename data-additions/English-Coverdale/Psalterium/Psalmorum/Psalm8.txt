8:2 O LORD our Governor, how excellent is thy Name in áll the wórld; * thou that hast set thy glóry above the héavens!
8:3 Out of the mouth of very babes and sucklings hast thou ordained strength, becáuse of thine énemies, * that thou mightest still the énemy and the avénger.
8:4 When I consider thy heavens, even the wórk of thy fíngers; * the moon and the stars which thóu hast ordáined;
8:5 What is man, that thou art míndful of hím? * and the son of man, that thou vísitest hím?
8:6 Thou madest him lówer than the ángels, * to crown him with glóry and wórship.
8:8a Thou makest him to have dominion of the wórks of thy hánds; * and thou hast put all things in subjéction under his féet:
8:8b All shéep and óxen; * yea, and the béasts of the fíeld;
8:9 The fowls of the air, and the físhes of the séa; * and whatsoever walketh through the páths of the séas.
8:10 O LÓRD our Góvernor, * how excellent is thy Name in áll the wórld!
