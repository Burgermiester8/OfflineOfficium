56:2 BE merciful unto me, O God, be merciful unto me; for my soul trústeth in thée; * and under the shadow of thy wings shall be my refuge, until this týranny be overpást.
56:3 I will call unto the Móst High Gód, * even unto the God that shall perform the cause which I háve in hánd.
56:4 He shall sénd from héaven, * and save me from the reproof of him that would éat me úp.
56:4 God shall send forth his mércy and trúth: * my sóul is among líons;
56:5 And I lie even among the children of men, that are sét on fíre, * whose teeth are spears and arrows, and their tóngue a sharp swórd.
56:6 Set up thyself, O God, abóve the héavens; * and thy glory abóve all the éarth.
56:7 They have laid a net for my feet, and pressed dówn my sóul; * they have digged a pit before me, and are fallen into the mídst of it themsélves.
56:8 My heart is fixed, O God, my héart is fíxed; * I will síng and give práise.
56:9 Awake up, my glory; awake, lúte and hárp: * I myself will awáke right éarly.
56:10 I will give thanks unto thee, O Lord, amóng the péoples; * and I will sing unto thee amóng the nátions.
56:11 For the greatness of thy mercy réacheth unto the héavens, * and thy trúth unto the clóuds.
56:12 Set up thyself, O God, abóve the héavens; * and thy glory above áll the éarth.
