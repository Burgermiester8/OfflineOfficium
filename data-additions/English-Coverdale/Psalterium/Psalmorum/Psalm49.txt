49:1 THE LORD, even the Most Mighty God, hath spoken, * and called the world, from the rising up of the sun unto the going down thereof.
49:1 Out of Sion hath God appeared * in perfect beauty.
49:3 Our God shall come, and shall not keep silence; * there shall go before him a consuming fire, and a mighty tempest shall be stirred up round about him.
49:4 He shall call the heaven from above, * and the earth, that he may judge his people.
49:5 Gather my saints together unto me; * those that have made a covenant with me with sacrifice.
49:6 And the heavens shall declare his righteousness; * for God is Judge himself.
49:7 Hear, O my people, and I will speak; * I myself will testify against thee, O Israel; for I am God, even thy God.
49:8 I will not reprove thee because of thy sacrifices; * as for thy burnt-offerings, they are alway before me.
49:9 I will take no bullock out of thine house, * nor he-goats out of thy folds.
49:10 For all the beasts of the forest are mine, * and so are the cattle upon a thousand hills.
49:11 I know all the fowls upon the mountains, * and the wild beasts of the field are in my sight.
49:12 If I be hungry, I will not tell thee; * for the whole world is mine, and all that is therein.
49:13 Thinkest thou that I will eat bulls’ flesh, * and drink the blood of goats?
49:14 Offer unto God thanksgiving, * and pay thy vows unto the Most Highest.
49:15 And call upon me in the time of trouble; * so will I hear thee, and thou shalt praise me.
49:16 But unto the ungodly saith God, * Why dost thou preach my laws, and takest my covenant in thy mouth;
49:17 Whereas thou hatest to be reformed, * and hast cast my words behind thee?
49:18 When thou sawest a thief, thou consentedst unto him; * and hast been partaker with the adulterers.
49:19 Thou hast let thy mouth speak wickedness, * and with thy tongue thou hast set forth deceit.
49:20 Thou sattest and spakest against thy brother; * yea, and hast slandered thine own mother’s son.
49:21 These things hast thou done, and I held my tongue, and thou thoughtest wickedly, that I am even such a one as thyself; * but I will reprove thee, and set before thee the things that thou hast done.
49:22 O consider this, ye that forget God, * lest I pluck you away, and there be none to deliver you.
49:23 Whoso offereth me thanks and praise, he honoreth me; * and to him that ordereth his way aright, will I show the salvation of God.
