42:1 GIVE sentence with me, O God, and defend my cause agáinst the ungódly péople; * O delíver me from the decéitful and wícked man.
42:2 For thou art the God of my strength; whý hast thou pút me fróm thee? * and why go I so héavily, while the énemy opprésseth me?
42:3 O send out thy light and thy trúth, that théy may léad me, * and bring me unto thy hóly hill, and tó thy dwélling;
42:4 And that I may go unto the altar of God, even unto the Gód of my jóy and gládness; * and upon the harp will I give thánks unto thée, O Gód, my God.
42:5 Why art thóu so héavy, O my sóul? * and why art thóu so disquíeted withín me?
42:6 O pút thy trúst in Gód; * for I will yet give him thanks, which is the hélp of my cóuntenance, and my Gód.
