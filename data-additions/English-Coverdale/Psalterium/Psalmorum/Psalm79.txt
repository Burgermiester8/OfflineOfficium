79:2 HEAR, O thou Shepherd of Israel, thou that leadest Joseph like a flock; * show thyself also, thou that sittest upon the Cherubim.
79:3 Before Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh, * stir up thy strength, and come and help us.
79:4 Turn us again, O God; * show the light of thy countenance, and we shall be whole.
79:5 O LORD God of hosts, * how long wilt thou be angry with thy people that prayeth?
79:6 Thou feedest them with the bread of tears, * and givest them plenteousness of tears to drink.
79:7 Thou hast made us a very strife unto our neighbors, * and our enemies laugh us to scorn.
79:8 Turn us again, thou God of hosts; * show the light of thy countenance, and we shall be whole.
79:9 Thou hast brought a vine out of Egypt; * thou hast cast out the heathen, and planted it.
79:10 Thou madest room for it; * and when it had taken root, it filled the land.
79:11 The hills were covered with the shadow of it, * and the boughs thereof were like the goodly cedar trees.
79:12 She stretched out her branches unto the sea, * and her boughs unto the River.
79:13 Why hast thou then broken down her hedge, * that all they that go by pluck off her grapes?
79:14 The wild boar out of the wood doth root it up, * and the wild beasts of the field devour it.
79:15 Turn thee again, thou God of hosts, look down from heaven, * behold, and visit this vine;
79:16 And the place of the vineyard that thy right hand hath planted, * and the branch that thou madest so strong for thyself.
79:17 It is burnt with fire, and cut down; * and they shall perish at the rebuke of thy countenance.
79:18 Let thy hand be upon the man of thy right hand, * and upon the son of man, whom thou madest so strong for thine own self.
79:19 And so will not we go back from thee: * O let us live, and we shall call upon thy Name.
79:20 Turn us again, O LORD God of hosts; * show the light of thy countenance, and we shall be whole.
