59:3 O GOD, thou hast cast us out, and scáttered us abróad; * thou hast also been displeased: O túrn thee unto us agáin.
59:4 Thou hast moved the lánd, and divíded it: * heal the sores thereóf, for it sháketh.
59:5 Thou hast showed thy people héavy thíngs; * thou hast given us a drink of déadly wíne.
59:6 Thou hast given a token for súch as féar thee, * that they may triumph bécause of the trúth.
59:7 Therefore were thy belóved delívered; * help me with thy right hánd, and héar me.
59:8 God hath spoken in his holiness, I will rejoice, and divíde Shéchem, * and mete out the válley of Súccoth.
59:9 Gilead is mine, and Manásseh is míne; * Ephraim also is the strength of my head; Júdah is my láwgiver;
59:9 Moab is my wash-pot; over Edom will I cást out my shóe; * Philistia, bé thou glád of me.
59:11 Who will léad me into the strong cíty? * who will bríng me into Édom?
59:12 Hast not thou cast us óut, O Gód? * wilt not thou, O God, go óut with our hósts?
59:13 O be thou our hélp in tróuble; * for vain is the hélp of mán.
59:14 Through God will we dó great ácts; * for it is he that shall tréad down our énemies.
