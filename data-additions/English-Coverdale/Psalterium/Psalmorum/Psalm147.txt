147:1 Praise the LÓRD, O Jerúsalem; * praise thy Gód, O Síon.
147:2 For he hath made fast the bárs of thy gátes, * and hath blessed thy chíldren withín thee.
147:3 He maketh péace in thy bórders, * and filleth thee with the flóur of whéat.
147:4 He sendeth forth his commándment upon éarth, * and his word rúnneth very swíftly.
147:5 He giveth snów like wóol, * and scattereth the hóarfrost like áshes.
147:6 He casteth forth his íce like mórsels: * who is able to abíde his fróst?
147:7 He sendeth out his word, and mélteth thém: * he bloweth with his wind, and the wáters flów.
147:8 He showeth his wórd unto Jácob, * his statutes and ordinances únto Ísrael.
147:9 He hath not dealt so with ány nátion; * neither have the heathen knówledge of his láws.
