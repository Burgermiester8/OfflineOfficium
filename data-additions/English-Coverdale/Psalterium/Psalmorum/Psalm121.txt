121:1 I WAS glad when they sáid unto mé, * We will go into the hóuse of the Lórd.
121:2 Our feet shall stánd in thy gátes, * O Jerúsalém.
121:3 Jerusalem is búilt as a cíty * that is at únity in itsélf.
121:4 For thither the tribes go up, even the tríbes of the Lórd, * to testify unto Israel, to give thanks unto the Náme of the Lórd.
121:5 For there is the seát of júdgment, * even the seat of the hoúse of Dávid.
121:6 O pray for the peace of Jerúsalém; * they shall prósper that lóve thee.
121:7 Peace be withín thy wálls, * and plenteousness withín thy pálaces.
121:8 For my brethren and compánions’ sákes, * I will wísh thee prospérity.
121:9 Yea, because of the house of the Lórd our Gód, * I will seek to dó thee goód.
