74:2 UNTO thee, O God, do wé give thánks; * yea, unto thee do wé give thánks.
74:3a Thy Name álso is so nígh; * and that do thy wondrous wórks decláre.
74:3b In the appointed time, sáith Gód, * I shall judge accórding unto ríght.
74:4 The earth is weak, and all the inhábiters thereóf: * I bear up the píllars of ít.
74:5 I said unto the fools, Deal nót so mádly; * and to the ungodly, Set not úp your hórn.
74:6 Set not up your hórn on hígh, * and speak not wíth a stiff néck.
74:7 For promotion cometh neither from the east, nor fróm the wést, * nor yét from the sóuth.
74:8a And why? Gód is the Júdge; * he putteth down one, and sétteth up anóther.
74:8b For in the hand of the LORD there is a cup, and the wíne is réd; * it is full mixed, and he poureth óut of the sáme.
74:9 As for the drégs thereóf, * all the ungodly of the earth shall drink them, and súck them óut.
74:10 But I will talk of the Gód of Jácob, * and práise him foréver.
74:11 All the horns of the ungodly álso will I bréak, * and the horns of the ríghteous shall be exálted.
