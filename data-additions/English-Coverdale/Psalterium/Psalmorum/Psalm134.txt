134:1 O PRAISE the LORD, laud ye the Náme of the LÓRD; * praise it, O ye servants óf the LÓRD;
134:2 Ye that stand in the hoúse of the LÓRD, * in the courts of the hoúse of our Gód.
134:3 O praise the LORD, for the LÓRD is grácious; * O sing praises unto his Name, for ít is lóvely.
134:4 For why? the LORD hath chosen Jacob untó himsélf, * and Israel for his ówn posséssion.
134:5 For I know that the LÓRD is greát, * and that our Lord is abóve all góds.
134:6 Whatsoever the LORD pleased, that did he in heaven, ánd in eárth; * and in the sea, and in áll deep pláces.
134:7a He bringeth forth the clouds from the énds of the wórld, * and sendeth forth lightnings with the rain, bringing the winds out of his treásuriés.
134:7b He smote the firstbórn of Égypt, * both of mán and beást.
134:9 He hath sent tokens and wonders into the midst of thee, O thou lánd of Égypt; * upon Pharaoh, and áll his sérvants.
134:10 He smote dívers nátions, * and slew míghty kíngs:
134:11 Sihon, king of the Amorites; and Og, the kíng of Báshan; * and all the kíngdoms of Cánaan;
134:12 And gave their land to be an héritáge, * even an heritage unto Israél his peóple.
134:13 Thy Name, O LORD, endúreth foréver; * so doth thy memorial, O LORD, from one generation tó anóther.
134:14 For the LORD will avénge his peóple, * and be gracious untó his sérvants.
134:15 As for the images of the heathen, they are but sílver and góld; * the wórk of men’s hánds.
134:16 They have mouths, ánd speak nót; * eyes have they, but théy see nót.
134:17 They have ears, and yet théy hear nót; * neither is there any breáth in their moúths.
134:18 They that make them are like únto thém; * and so are all they that put their trúst in thém.
134:19 Praise the LORD, ye house of Ísraél; * praise the LORD, ye hoúse of Aáron.
134:20 Praise the LORD, ye hoúse of Lévi; * ye that fear the LORD, praíse the LÓRD.
134:21 Praised be the LORD oút of Síon, * who dwelleth at Jerúsalém.
