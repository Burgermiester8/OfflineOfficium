100:1 MY song shall be of mercy and judgment; * unto thee, O LORD, will I sing.
100:2 O let me have understanding * in the way of godliness!
100:2 When wilt thou come unto me? * I will walk in my house with a perfect heart.
100:3 I will take no wicked thing in hand; I hate the sins of unfaithfulness; * there shall no such cleave unto me.
100:4 A froward heart shall depart from me; * I will not know a wicked person.
100:5 Whoso privily slandereth his neighbor, * him will I destroy.
100:5 Whoso hath also a haughty look and a proud heart, * I will not suffer him.
100:6 Mine eyes look upon such as are faithful in the land, * that they may dwell with me.
100:7a Whoso leadeth a godly life, * he shall be my servant.
100:7b There shall no deceitful person dwell in my house; * he that telleth lies shall not tarry in my sight.
100:8 I shall soon destroy all the ungodly that are in the land; * that I may root out all wicked doers from the city of the LORD.
