131:1 LORD, remémber Dávid, * and all his trouble:
131:2 How he sware untó the LÓRD, * and vowed a vow unto the Almighty Gód of Jácob:
131:3 I will not come within the tabernacle óf mine hoúse, * nor climb up ínto my béd;
131:4 I will not suffer mine eyes to sleep, nor mine éyelids to slúmber; * neither the temples of my head to take ány rést;
131:5 Until I find out a place for the temple óf the LÓRD; * an habitation for the Mighty Gód of Jácob.
131:6 Lo, we heard of the sáme at Ephrátah, * and found it ín the woód.
131:7 We will go into his tábernácle, * and fall low on our knees befóre his foótstool.
131:8 Arise, O LORD, into thy résting-pláce; * thou, and the árk of thy stréngth.
131:9 Let thy priests be clothed with ríghteousnéss; * and let thy saints sing with jóyfulnéss.
131:10 For thy servant Dávid’s sáke, * turn not away the face of thíne anoínted.
131:11a The LORD hath made a faithful oath únto Dávid, * and he shall not shrínk from ít:
131:11b Of the fruít of thy bódy * shall I set upón thy thróne.
131:12 If thy children will keep my covenant, and my testimonies that Í shall teách them; * their children also shall sit upon thy throne forévermóre.
131:13 For the LORD hath chosen Sion to be an habitation fór himsélf; * he hath lónged for hér.
131:14 This shall be my rést foréver: * here will I dwell, for I have a delíght thereín.
131:15 I will bless her victuáls with íncrease, * and will satisfy her poór with breád.
131:16 I will deck her priésts with heálth, * and her saints shall rejoíce and síng.
131:17 There shall I make the horn of Dávid to floúrish: * I have ordained a lantern for míne anoínted.
131:18 As for his enemies, I shall clóthe them with sháme; * but upon himself sháll his crown floúrish.
