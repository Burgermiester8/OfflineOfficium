65:1 O BE joyful in God, áll ye lánds; * sing praises unto the honor of his Name; make his práise to be glórious.
65:3 Say unto God, O how wonderful art thóu in thy wórks! * through the greatness of thy power shall thine enemies bow dówn unto thée.
65:4 For all the world shall wórship thée, * sing of thee, and práise thy Náme.
65:5 O come hither, and behold the wórks of Gód; * how wonderful he is in his doing toward the chíldren of mén.
65:6 He turned the séa into dry lánd, * so that they went through the water on foot; there did we rejóice thereóf.
65:7 He ruleth with his power forever; his eyes behóld the nátions: * and such as will not believe shall not be able to exált themsélves.
65:8 O praise our Gód, ye péoples, * and make the voice of his práise to be héard;
65:9 Who holdeth our sóul in lífe; * and suffereth not our féet to slíp.
65:10 For thou, O Gód, hast próved us; * thou also hast tried us, like as sílver is tríed.
65:11 Thou broughtest us ínto the snáre; * and laidest trouble upón our lóins.
65:12 Thou sufferedst men to ride óver our héads; * we went through fire and water, and thou broughtest us out into a wéalthy pláce.
65:13 I will go into thine house with burnt-offerings, and will páy thee my vóws, * which I promised with my lips, and spake with my mouth, when Í was in tróuble.
65:15 I will offer unto thee fat burnt-sacrifices, with the íncense of ráms; * I will offer búllocks and góats.
65:16 O come hither, and harken, all yé that fear Gód; * and I will tell you what he hath dóne for my sóul.
65:17 I called unto hím with my móuth, * and gave him práises with my tóngue.
65:18 If I incline unto wíckedness with mine héart, * the Lórd will not héar me.
65:19 But Gód hath héard me; * and considered the vóice of my práyer.
65:20 Praised be God, who hath not cast óut my práyer, * nor turned his mércy from mé.
