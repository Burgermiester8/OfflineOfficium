107:2 O GOD, my heart is ready, my heárt is reády; * I will sing, and give praise with the best member thát I háve.
107:3 Awake, thou lúte and hárp; * I myself will awáke right eárly.
107:4 I will give thanks unto thee, O LORD, amóng the peóples; * I will sing praises unto thee amóng the nátions.
107:5 For thy mercy is greater thán the heávens, * and thy truth reacheth untó the cloúds.
107:6 Set up thyself, O God, abóve the heávens, * and thy glory above áll the eárth;
107:7 That thy beloved may bé delívered: * let thy right hand save them, and heár thou mé.
107:8 God hath spoken in his hólinéss; * I will rejoice therefore, and divide Shechem, † and mete out the válley of Súccoth.
107:9 Gilead is mine, and Manásseh is míne; * Ephraim also is the strength of my head; † Judah ís my láwgiver;
107:10 Moab is my wash-pot; † over Edom will I cast oút my shoé; * upon Philistia wíll I tríumph.
107:11 Who will lead me into thé strong cíty? * and who will bring me ínto Édom?
107:12 Hast not thou forsaken ús, O Gód? * and wilt not thou, O God, go fórth with our hósts?
107:13 O help us agaínst the énemy: * for vain is the hélp of mán.
107:14 Through God we shall dó great ácts; * and it is he that shall tread down our énemiés.
