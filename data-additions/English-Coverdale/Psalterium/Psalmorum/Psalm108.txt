108:2 HOLD not thy tongue, O Gód of my praíse; * for the mouth of the ungodly, yea, the mouth of the deceitful is ópened upón me.
108:3 And they have spoken against me wíth false tóngues; * they compassed me about also with words of hatred, † and fought against me withóut a cáuse.
108:4 For the love that I had unto them, lo, they take now my cóntrary párt; * but I give myself únto práyer.
108:5 Thus have they rewarded me évil for goód, * and hatred for mÿ good wíll.
108:6 Set thou an ungodly man to be rúler óver him, * and let an adversary stand at hís right hánd.
108:7 When sentence is given upon him, let him bé condémned; * and let his prayer be túrned into sín.
108:8 Let his dáys be féw; * and let another táke his óffice.
108:9 Let his children be fátherléss, * and his wífe a wídow.
108:10 Let his children be vagabonds, and bég their breád; * let them seek it also out of désolate pláces.
108:11 Let the extortioner consume áll that he háth; * and let the stranger spoíl his lábor.
108:12 Let there be no man to píty hím, * nor to have compassion upon his fátherless chíldren.
108:13 Let his posterity bé destróyed; * and in the next generation let his name be cleán put oút.
108:14 Let the wickedness of his fathers be had in remembrance in the síght of the LÓRD; * and let not the sin of his mother be dóne awáy.
108:15 Let them alway be befóre the LÓRD, * that he may root out the memorial of them from óff the eárth;
108:17 And that, because his mind was nót to do goód; * but persecuted the poor helpless man, † that he might slay him that was véxed at the heárt.
108:18 His delight was in cursing, and it shall happen únto hím; * he loved not blessing, therefore shall it be fár from hím.
108:18 He clothed himself with cursing like as wíth a raíment, * and it shall come into his bowels like water, † and like oil ínto his bónes.
108:19 Let it be unto him as the cloak that he háth upón him, * and as the girdle that he is alway gírded withál.
108:20 Let it thus happen from the LORD unto mine énemiés, * and to those that speak evil agaínst my soúl.
108:21 But deal thou with me, O LORD God, according únto thy Náme; * for sweét is thy mércy.
108:22 O deliver me, for I am hélpless and poór, * and my heart is woúnded withín me.
108:23 I go hence like the shadow thát depárteth, * and am driven awáy as the grásshopper.
108:24 My knees are weák through fásting; * my flesh is dried up for wánt of fátness.
108:25 I am become also a reproách unto thém: * they that look upon me sháke their heáds.
108:26 Help me, O LÓRD my Gód; * O save me according tó thy mércy;
108:27 And they shall know how that thís is thy hánd, * and that thou, LÓRD, hast dóne it.
108:28 Though they cúrse, yet bléss thou; * and let them be confounded that rise up against me; † but let thy sérvant rejoíce.
108:29 Let mine adversaries be clóthed with sháme; * and let them cover themselves with their own confusion, as wíth a cloák.
108:30 As for me, I will give great thanks unto the LÓRD with my moúth, * and praise him among the múltitúde;
108:31 For he shall stand at the right hánd of the poór, * to save his soul from unríghteous júdges.
