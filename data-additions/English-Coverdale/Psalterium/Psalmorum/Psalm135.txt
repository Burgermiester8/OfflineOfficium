135:1 O GIVE thanks unto the LORD, for hé is grácious: * and his mercy endúreth foréver.
135:2 O give thanks unto the Gód of all góds: * for his mercy endúreth foréver.
135:3 O thank the Lórd of all lórds: * for his mercy endúreth foréver.
135:4 Who only dóeth great wónders: * for his mercy endúreth foréver.
135:5 Who by his excellent wísdom made the héavens: * for his mercy endúreth foréver.
135:6 Who laid out the earth abóve the wáters: * for his mercy endúreth foréver.
135:7 Who hath máde great líghts: * for his mercy endúreth foréver:
135:8 The sun to rúle the dáy: * for his mercy endúreth foréver;
135:9 The moon and the stars to góvern the níght: * for his mercy endúreth foréver.
135:10 Who smote Égypt, with their fírstborn: * for his mercy endúreth foréver;
135:11 And brought out Ísrael from amóng them: * for his mercy endúreth foréver;
135:12 With a mighty hand and strétched-out árm: * for his mercy endúreth foréver.
135:13 Who divided the Red Séa in two párts: * for his mercy endúreth foréver;
135:14 And made Israel to gó through the mídst of it: * for his mercy endúreth foréver.
135:15 But as for Pharaoh and his host, † he overthrew them ín the Red Séa: * for his mercy endúreth foréver.
135:16 Who led his péople through the wílderness: * for his mercy endúreth foréver.
135:17 Who smóte great kíngs: * for his mercy endúreth foréver;
135:18 Yea, and sléw mighty kíngs: * for his mercy endúreth foréver:
135:19 Sihon, kíng of the Ámorites: * for his mercy endúreth foréver;
135:20 And Og, the kíng of Báshan: * for his mercy endúreth foréver;
135:21 And gave away their lánd for an héritage: * for his mercy endúreth foréver;
135:22 Even for an heritage unto Ísrael his sérvant: * for his mercy endúreth foréver.
135:23 Who remembered us whén we were in troúble: * for his mercy endúreth foréver;
135:24 And hath delivered us fróm our énemies: * for his mercy endúreth foréver.
135:25 Who giveth fóod to all flésh: * for his mercy endúreth foréver.
135:26a O give thanks unto the Gód of héaven: * for his mercy endúreth foréver.
135:26b O give thanks unto the Lórd of lórds: * for his mercy endúreth foréver.
