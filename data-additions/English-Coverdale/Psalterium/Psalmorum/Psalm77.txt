77:1 HEAR my law, Ó my péople; * incline your ears unto the wórds of my móuth.
77:2 I will open my móuth in a párable; * I will declare hard séntences of óld;
77:3 Which we have héard and knówn, * and such as our fáthers have tóld us;
77:4 That we should not hide them from the children of the generátions to cóme; * but to show the honor of the LORD, his mighty and wonderful works that hé hath dóne.
77:5 He made a covenant with Jacob, and gave Ísrael a láw, * which he commanded our forefathers to téach their chíldren;
77:5 That their postérity might knów it, * and the children which were yét unbórn;
77:6 To the intent that whén they came úp, * they might show their chíldren the sáme;
77:7 That they might put their trúst in Gód; * and not to forget the works of God, but to kéep his commándments;
77:8 And not to be as their forefathers, a faithless and stúbborn generátion; * a generation that set not their heart aright, and whose spirit clave not stedfástly unto Gód;
77:9 Like as the chíldren of Éphraim; * who being harnessed, and carrying bows, turned themselves back in the dáy of báttle.
77:10 They kept not the cóvenant of Gód, * and would not wálk in his láw;
77:11 But forgat what hé had dóne, * and the wonderful works that he had shówed for thém.
77:12 Marvelous things did he in the sight of our forefathers, in the lánd of Égypt, * even in the fíeld of Zóan.
77:13 He divided the sea, and lét them go thróugh; * he made the waters to stánd on an héap.
77:14 In the daytime also he léd them with a clóud, * and all the night through with a líght of fíre.
77:15 He clave the hard rócks in the wílderness, * and gave them drink thereof, as it had been óut of the great dépth.
77:16 He brought waters out of the stóny róck, * so that it gushed óut like the rívers.
77:17 Yet for all this they sinned móre agáinst him, * and provoked the Most Híghest in the wílderness.
77:18 They tempted Gód in their héarts, * and required méat for their lúst.
77:19 They spake against God álso, sáying, * Shall God prepare a táble in the wílderness?
77:20 He smote the stony rock indeed, that the water gushed out, and the stréams flowed withál; * but can he give bread also, or provide flésh for his péople?
77:21 When the LORD heard this, hé was wróth; * so the fire was kindled in Jacob, and there came up heavy displéasure against Ísrael;
77:22 Because they believed nót in Gód, * and put not their trúst in his hélp.
77:23 So he commanded the clóuds abóve, * and opened the dóors of héaven.
77:24 He rained down manna also upon them fór to éat, * and gave them fóod from héaven.
77:25 So man did eat ángels’ fóod; * for he sént them meat enóugh.
77:26 He caused the east-wind to blów under héaven; * and through his power he brought in the sóuthwest wínd.
77:27 He rained flesh upon them as thíck as dúst, * and feathered fowls like as the sánd of the séa.
77:28 He let it fall amóng their ténts, * even round about their hábitátion.
77:29 So they did éat, and were well fílled; for he gave them their ówn desíre: * they were not disappóinted of their lúst.
77:30 But while the meat was yet in their mouths, the heavy wrath of God came upon them, and slew the wéalthiest of thém; * yea, and smote down the chosen men that wére in Ísrael.
77:32 But for all this they sínned yet móre, * and believed not his wóndrous wórks.
77:33 Therefore their days did he consúme in vánity, * and their yéars in tróuble.
77:34 When he sléw them, they sóught him, * and turned them early, and inquíred after Gód.
77:35 And they remembered that Gód was their stréngth, * and that the High God was théir redéemer.
77:36 Nevertheless, they did but flatter him with their móuth, * and dissembled with hím in their tóngue.
77:37 For their heart was not whóle with hím, * neither continued they stéadfast in his cóvenant.
77:38 But he was so merciful, that he forgáve their misdéeds, * and destróyed them nót.
77:38 Yea, many a time turned he his wráth awáy, * and would not suffer his whole displeasure tó aríse.
77:39 For he considered that théy were but flésh, * and that they were even a wind that passeth away, and cometh nót agáin.
77:40 MANY a time did they provóke him in the wílderness, * and gríeved him in the désert.
77:41 They turned back, and témpted Gód, * and provoked the Holy Óne in Ísrael.
77:42 They thought not óf his hánd, * and of the day when he delivered them from the hánd of the énemy;
77:43 How he had wrought his míracles in Égypt, * and his wonders in the fíeld of Zóan.
77:44 He turned their wáters into blóod, * so that they might not drínk of the rívers.
77:45 He sent flies among them, and devóured them úp; * and frógs to destróy them.
77:46 He gave their fruit únto the cáterpillar, * and their labor únto the grásshopper.
77:47 He destroyed their vínes with háilstones, * and their mulberry trees wíth the fróst.
77:48 He smote their cattle álso with háilstones, * and their flócks with hot thúnderbolts.
77:49 He cast upon them the furiousness of his wrath, anger, displéasure, and tróuble: * and sent evil ángels amóng them.
77:50 He made a way to his indignation, and spared not their sóul from déath; * but gave their life óver to the péstilence;
77:51 And smote all the firstbórn in Égypt, * the most principal and mightiest in the dwéllings of Hám.
77:52 But as for his own people, he led them fórth like shéep, * and carried them in the wílderness like a flóck.
77:53 He brought them out safely, that they shóuld not féar, * and overwhelmed their énemies with the séa.
77:54 And brought them within the bórders of his sánctuary, * even to this mountain, which he purchased wíth his right hánd.
77:54 He cast out the heathen álso befóre them, * caused their land to be divided among them for an heritage, and made the tribes of Israel to dwéll in their ténts.
77:56 Yet they tempted and displeased the Móst High Gód, * and kept nót his téstimonies.
77:57 They turned their backs, and fell away líke their fórefathers; * starting aside like a bróken bów.
77:58 For they grieved him wíth their híll-altars, * and provoked him to displéasure with their ímages.
77:59 When God heard this, hé was wróth, * and took sore displéasure at Ísrael;
77:60 So that he forsook the tábernacle in Shíloh, * even the tent that he had pítched among mén.
77:61 He delivered their pówer into captívity, * and their beauty into the énemy’s hánd
77:62 He gave his people over also únto the swórd, * and was wróth with his inhéritance.
77:63 The fire consúmed their young mén, * and their maidens were not gíven in márriage.
77:64 Their priests were sláin with the swórd, * and there were no widows to máke lamentátion.
77:65 So the Lord awaked as one óut of sléep, * and like a giant refréshed with wíne.
77:66 He drave his énemies báckward, * and put them to a perpétual sháme.
77:67 He refused the tábernacle of Jóseph, * and chose not the tríbe of Éphraim;
77:68 But chose the tríbe of Júdah, * even the hill of Síon which he lóved.
77:69 And there he built his témple on hígh, * and laid the foundation of it like the ground which he hath made contínually.
77:70 He chose David álso his sérvant, * and took him awáy from the shéep-folds:
77:71 As he was following the ewes with their yóung he tóok him, * that he might feed Jacob his people, and Ísrael his inhéritance.
77:72 So he fed them with a fáithful and true héart, * and ruled them prudently with áll his pówer.
