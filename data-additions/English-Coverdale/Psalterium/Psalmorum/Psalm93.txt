93:1 O LORD God, to whom vengeance belongeth, * thou God, to whom vengeance belongeth, show thyself.
93:2 Arise, thou Judge of the world, * and reward the proud after their deserving.
93:3 LORD, how long shall the ungodly, * how long shall the ungodly triumph?
93:4 How long shall all wicked doers speak so disdainfully, * and make such proud boasting?
93:5 They smite down thy people, O LORD, * and trouble thine heritage.
93:6 They murder the widow and the stranger, * and put the fatherless to death.
93:7 And yet they say, Tush, the LORD shall not see, * neither shall the God of Jacob regard it.
93:8 Take heed, ye unwise among the people: * O ye fools, when will ye understand?
93:9 He that planted the ear, shall he not hear? * or he that made the eye, shall he not see?
93:10 Or he that instructeth the heathen, * it is he that teacheth man knowledge; shall not he punish?
93:11 The LORD knoweth the thoughts of man, * that they are but vain.
93:12 Blessed is the man whom thou chastenest, O LORD, * and teachest him in thy law;
93:13 That thou mayest give him patience in time of adversity, * until the pit be digged up for the ungodly.
93:14 For the LORD will not fail his people; * neither will he forsake his inheritance;
93:15 Until righteousness turn again unto judgment: * all such as are true in heart shall follow it.
93:16 Who will rise up with me against the wicked? * or who will take my part against the evil doers?
93:17 If the LORD had not helped me, * it had not failed, but my soul had been put to silence.
93:18 But when I said, My foot hath slipped; * thy mercy, O LORD, held me up.
93:19 In the multitude of the sorrows that I had in my heart, * thy comforts have refreshed my soul.
93:20 Wilt thou have any thing to do with the throne of wickedness, * which imagineth mischief as a law?
93:21 They gather them together against the soul of the righteous, * and condemn the innocent blood.
93:22 But the LORD is my refuge, * and my God is the strength of my confidence.
93:23 He shall recompense them their wickedness, and destroy them in their own malice; * yea, the LORD our God shall destroy them.
