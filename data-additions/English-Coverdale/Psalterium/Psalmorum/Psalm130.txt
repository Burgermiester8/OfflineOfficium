130:1a O Lord, I am nót high-mínded; * I have no próud loóks.
130:1b I do not exercise myself in gréat mátters * which are too hígh for mé.
130:2 But I refrain my soul, and keep it low, † like as a child that is wéaned from his móther: * yea, my soul is éven as a wéaned child.
130:3 O Israel, trúst in the Lórd * from this time forth for évermóre.
