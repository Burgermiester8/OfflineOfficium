38:2 I SAID, I will take héed to my wáys, * that I offend nót in my tóngue.
38:2 I will keep my mouth as it wére with a brídle, * while the ungódly is ín my síght.
38:3 I held my tongue, and spáke nóthing: * I kept silence, yea, even from good words; but it was páin and gríef to me.
38:4 My heart was hot within me: and while I was thus músing the fíre kíndled, * and at the last I spáke with my tóngue:
38:5 LORD, let me know mine end, and the númber of my dáys; * that I may be certified how lóng I háve to líve.
38:6 Behold, thou hast made my days as it were a span long, and mine age is even as nóthing in respéct of thée; * and verily every man living is altogéther vánity.
38:7 For man walketh in a vain shadow, and disquieteth himsélf in váin; * he heapeth up riches, and cannot tell whó shall gáther them.
38:8 And now, Lord, whát is my hópe? * truly my hópe is éven in thée.
38:9 Deliver me from áll mine offénces; * and make me not a rebúke unto the fóolish.
38:10 I became dumb, and ópened nót my móuth; * for ít was thy dóing.
38:12 Take thy plague awáy from mé: * I am even consumed by the méans of thy héavy hánd.
38:12 When thou with rebukes dost chasten man for sin, thou makest his beauty to consume away, like as it were a móth frétting a gárment: * every man thérefore is bút vánity.
38:13 Hear my prayer, O LORD, and with thine ears consíder my cálling; * hold not thy péace at my téars;
38:13 For I am a stranger with thée, and a sojóurner, * as áll my fáthers were.
38:14 O spare me a little, that I may recóver my stréngth, * before I go hence, and be nó more séen.
