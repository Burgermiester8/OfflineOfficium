68:2 SÁVE me, O Gód; * for the waters are come in, even únto my sóul.
68:3 I stick fast in the deep míre, where no gróund is; * I am come into deep waters, so that the flóods run óver me.
68:4 I am weary of crying; my thróat is drý; * my sight faileth me for waiting so long upón my Gód.
68:5 They that hate me without a cause are more than the háirs of my héad; * they that are mine enemies, and would destroy me gúiltless, are míghty.
68:6 I paid them the things that I néver tóok: * God, thou knowest my simpleness, and my faults are not híd from thée.
68:7 Let not them that trust in thee, O Lord GOD of hosts, be ashámed for my cáuse; * let not those that seek thee be confounded through me, O Lord Gód of Ísrael.
68:8 And why? for thy sake have I súffered repróof; * shame hath cóvered my fáce.
68:9 I am become a stranger únto my bréthren, * even an alien unto my móther’s chíldren.
68:10 For the zeal of thine house hath éven éaten me; * and the rebukes of them that rebuked thee are fállen upón me.
68:11 I wept, and chastened mysélf with fásting, * and that was túrned to my repróof.
68:12 I put on sáckcloth also, * and they jésted upón me.
68:13 They that sit in the gate spéak agáinst me, * and the drunkards make sóngs upón me.
68:13 But, LORD, I make my práyer unto thée * in an accéptable tíme.
68:14 Hear me, O God, in the múltitude of thy mércy, * even in the trúth of thy salvátion.
68:15 Take me out of the míre, that I sínk not; * O let me be delivered from them that hate me, and óut of the deep wáters.
68:16 Let not the waterflood drown me, neither let the deep swállow me úp; * and let not the pit shut her móuth upón me.
68:17 Hear me, O LORD, for thy loving-kíndness is cómfortable; * turn thee unto me according to the múltitude of thy mércies.
68:18 And hide not thy face from thy servant; for Í am in tróuble: * O háste thee, and héar me.
68:19 Draw nigh unto my sóul, and sáve it; * O deliver me, becáuse of mine énemies.
68:20 Thou hast known my reproach, my sháme, and my díshonor: * mine adversaries are áll in thy síght.
68:21 Reproach hath broken my heart; I am fúll of héaviness: * I looked for some to have pity on me, but there was no man, neither found I ány to cómfort me.
68:22 They gave me gáll to éat; * and when I was thirsty they gave me vínegar to drínk.
68:23 Let their table be made a snare to take themsélves withál; * and let the things that should have been for their wealth be unto them an occásion of fálling.
68:24 Let their eyes be blinded, thát they see nót; * and ever bow thou dówn their bácks.
68:25 Pour out thine indignátion upón them, * and let thy wrathful displéasure take hóld of them.
68:26 Let their habitátion be vóid, * and no man to dwéll in their ténts.
68:27 For they persecute him whom thóu hast smítten; * and they talk how they may vex them whom thóu hast wóunded.
68:28 Let them fall from one wíckedness to anóther, * and not come ínto thy ríghteousness.
68:29 Let them be wiped out of the bóok of the líving, * and not be wrítten among the ríghteous.
68:30 As for me, when I am póor and in héaviness, * thy help, O God, shall líft me úp.
68:31 I will praise the Name of Gód with a sóng, * and magnify it wíth thanksgíving.
68:32 This also shall pléase the LÓRD * better than a bullock that hath hórns and hóofs.
68:33 The humble shall consider thís, and be glád: * seek ye after God, and your sóul shall líve.
68:34 For the LORD héareth the póor, * and despiseth nót his prísoners.
68:35 Let heaven and éarth práise him: * the sea, and all that móveth thérein.
68:36 For God will save Sion, and build the cíties of Júdah, * that men may dwell there, and have it ín posséssion.
68:37 The posterity also of his servants sháll inhérit it; * and they that love his Name shall dwéll theréin.
