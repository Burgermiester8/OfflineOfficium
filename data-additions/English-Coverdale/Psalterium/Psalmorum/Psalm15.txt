15:1 PRESERVE mé, O Gód; * for in thee have I pút my trúst.
15:2 O my soul, thou hast sáid unto the LÓRD, * Thou art my God; I have no good líke unto thée.
15:3 All my delight is upon the sáints that are in the éarth, * and upon such as excél in vírtue.
15:4a But they that run after anóther gód * shall háve great tróuble.
15:4b Their drink-offerings of blóod will I not óffer, * neither make mention of their námes within my líps.
15:5 The LORD himself is the portion of mine inhéritance, and of my cúp; * thou shalt maintáin my lót.
15:6 The lot is fallen unto mé in a fair gróund; * yea, I have a góodly héritage.
15:7 I will thank the LORD for gíving me wárning; * my reins also chasten me ín the night séason.
15:8 I have set the LORD álway befóre me; * for he is on my right hand, therefore I sháll not fáll.
15:9 Wherefore my heart is glad, and my glóry rejóiceth: * my flesh also shall rést in hópe.
15:10 For why? thou shalt not leave my sóul in héll; * neither shalt thou suffer thy Holy One to sée corrúption.
15:11 Thou shalt show me the path of life: in thy presence is the fúllness of jóy, * and at thy right hand there is pléasure forévermore.
