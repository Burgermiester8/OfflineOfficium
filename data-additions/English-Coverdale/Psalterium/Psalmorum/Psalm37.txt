37:2 PUT me not to rebuke, O LORD, ín thine ánger; * neither chasten me in thy héavy displéasure:
37:3 For thine arrows stick fást in mé, * and thy hand présseth me sóre.
37:4 There is no health in my flesh, because of thý displéasure; * neither is there any rest in my bones, by réason óf my sin.
37:5 For my wickednesses are gone óver my héad, * and are like a sore burden, too héavy for mé to bear.
37:6 My wounds stink, and áre corrúpt, * thróugh my fóolishness.
37:7 I am brought into so great tróuble and mísery, * that I go mourning áll the dáy long.
37:8 For my loins are filled with a sóre diséase, * and there is no whole párt in my bódy.
37:9 I am féeble and sore smítten; * I have roared for the very disquíetness óf my heart.
37:10 Lord, thou knowest áll my désire; * and my groaning ís not híd from thee.
37:11 My heart panteth, my stréngth hath fáiled me, * and the light of mine éyes is góne from me.
37:12 My lovers and my neighbors did stand looking upón my tróuble, * and my kínsmen stóod afar off.
37:13 They also that sought after my lífe laid snáres for me; * and they that went about to do me evil talked of wickedness, and imagined decéit all the dáy long.
37:14 As for me, I was like a deaf mán, and héard not; * and as one that is dumb, who doth not ópen his móuth.
37:15 I became even as a mán that héareth not, * and in whose móuth are nó reproofs.
37:16 For in thee, O LORD, have I pút my trúst; * thou shalt answer for me, O Lórd my Gód.
37:17 I have required that they, even mine enemies, should not tríumph óver me; * for when my foot slipped, they rejoíced greatly agáinst me.
37:18 And I truly am sét in the plágue, * and my heaviness is éver ín my sight.
37:19 For I will conféss my wíckedness, * and be sórry fór my sin.
37:20 But mine enemies líve, and are míghty; * and they that hate me wrongfully are mány in númber.
37:21 They also that reward evil for good áre agáinst me; * because I follow the thíng that góod is.
37:22 Forsake me not, O LÓRD my Gód; * be not thóu fár from me.
37:23 Haste thée to hélp me, * O Lord God of mý salvátion.
