45:2 GOD is our hope and strength, * a very present help in trouble.
45:3 Therefore will we not fear, though the earth be moved, * and though the hills be carried into the midst of the sea;
45:4 Though the waters thereof rage and swell, * and though the mountains shake at the tempest of the same.
45:5 There is a river, the streams whereof make glad the city of God; * the holy place of the tabernacle of the Most Highest.
45:6 God is in the midst of her, therefore shall she not be removed; * God shall help her, and that right early.
45:7 The nations make much ado, and the kingdoms are moved; * but God hath showed his voice, and the earth shall melt away.
45:8 The LORD of hosts is with us; * the God of Jacob is our refuge.
45:9 O come hither, and behold the works of the LORD, * what destruction he hath brought upon the earth.
45:10 He maketh wars to cease in all the world; * he breaketh the bow, and knappeth the spear in sunder, and burneth the chariots in the fire.
45:11 Be still then, and know that I am God: * I will be exalted among the nations, and I will be exalted in the earth.
45:12 The LORD of hosts is with us; * the God of Jacob is our refuge.
