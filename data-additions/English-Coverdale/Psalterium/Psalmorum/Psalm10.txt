10:2 IN the LORD put I my trúst; † how say ye thén to my sóul, * that she should flee as a bírd unto the híll?
10:3 For lo, the ungodly bend their bow, and make ready their árrows within the qúiver, * that they may privily shoot at them which are trúe of héart.
10:4 If the foundátions be destróyed, * what can the ríghteous dó?
10:5a The LORD is in his hóly témple; * the LORD’s séat is in héaven.
10:5b His eyes consíder the póor, * and his eyelids try the chíldren of mén.
10:6 The LORD appróveth the ríghteous: * but the ungodly, and him that delighteth in wickedness, dóth his soul ábhor.
10:7 Upon the ungodly he shall rain snares, fire and brimstone, stórm and témpest: * this shall be their pórtion to drínk.
10:8 For the righteous LORD lóveth ríghteousness; * his countenance will behold the thíng that is júst.
