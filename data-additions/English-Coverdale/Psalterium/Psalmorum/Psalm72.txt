72:1 TRULY God is lóving unto Ísrael: * even unto such as are óf a clean héart.
72:2 Nevertheless, my feet were álmost góne, * my treadings had wéll-nigh slípped.
72:3 And why? I was gríeved at the wícked: * I do also see the ungódly in such prospérity.
72:4 For they are in no péril of déath; * but are lústy and stróng.
72:5 They come in no misfortune like óther fólk; * neither are they plagued like óther mén.
72:6 And this is the cause that they are so hólden with príde, * and cruelty covereth them ás a gárment.
72:7 Their eyes swéll with fátness, * and they do éven what they lúst.
72:8 They corrupt other, and speak of wícked blásphemy; * their talking is agáinst the Most Hígh.
72:9 For they stretch forth their mouth únto the héaven, * and their tongue goeth thróugh the wórld.
72:10 Therefore fall the people únto thém, * and thereout suck they no smáll advántage.
72:11 Tush, say they, how should Gód percéive it? * is there knówledge in the Most Hígh?
72:12 Lo, thése are the ungódly, * these prosper in the world, and these have ríches in posséssion:
72:13 And I said, Then have I cleansed my héart in váin, * and washed my hánds in ínnocency.
72:14 All the day long have Í been púnished, * and chastened évery mórning.
72:15 Yea, and I had almost said éven as théy; * but lo, then I should have condemned the generátion of thy chíldren.
72:16 Then thought Í to understánd this; * but ít was too hárd for me,
72:17 Until I went into the sánctuary of Gód: * then understood I the énd of these mén;
72:18 Namely, how thou dost set them in slíppery pláces, * and castest them down, and destróyest thém.
72:19 O how súddenly do they consúme, * perish, and cóme to a féarful end!
72:20 Yea, even like as a dream when óne awáketh; * so shalt thou make their image to vanish óut of the cíty.
72:21 Thus my héart was gríeved, * and it went even thróugh my réins.
72:23 So foolish was Í, and ígnorant, * even as it were a béast befóre thee.
72:24a Nevertheless, I am álway bý thee; * for thou hast hólden me by my ríght hand.
72:24b Thou shalt gúide me with thy cóunsel, * and after that recéive me with glóry.
72:25 Whom have I in héaven but thée? * and there is none upon earth that I desire in compárison of thée.
72:26 My flésh and my heart fáileth; * but God is the strength of my heart, and my pórtion foréver.
72:27 For lo, they that forsáke thee shall pérish; * thou hast destroyed all them that are unfáithful unto thée.
72:28 But it is good for me to hold me fast by God, to put my trúst in the Lord GÓD, * and to speak of all thy works in the gates of the dáughter of Síon.
