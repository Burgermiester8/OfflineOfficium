80:2 SING we merrily unto God our strength; * make a cheerful noise unto the God of Jacob.
80:3 Take the psalm, bring hither the tabret, * the merry harp with the lute.
80:4 Blow up the trumpet in the new moon, * even in the time appointed, and upon our solemn feast-day.
80:5 For this was made a statute for Israel, * and a law of the God of Jacob.
80:6 This he ordained in Joseph for a testimony, * when he came out of the land of Egypt, and had heard a strange language.
80:7 I eased his shoulder from the burden, * and his hands were delivered from making the pots.
80:8a Thou calledst upon me in troubles, and I delivered thee; * and heard thee what time as the storm fell upon thee.
80:8b I proved thee also * at the waters of strife.
80:9a Hear, O my people; and I will assure thee, O Israel, * if thou wilt harken unto me,
80:9b There shall no strange god be in thee, * neither shalt thou worship any other god.
80:11 I am the LORD thy God, who brought thee out of the land of Egypt: * open thy mouth wide, and I shall fill it.
80:12 But my people would not hear my voice; * and Israel would not obey me;
80:13 So I gave them up unto their own hearts’ lusts, * and let them follow their own imaginations.
80:14 O that my people would have harkened unto me! * for if Israel had walked in my ways,
80:15 I should soon have put down their enemies, * and turned my hand against their adversaries.
80:16 The haters of the LORD should have submitted themselves unto him; * but their time should have endured forever.
80:17 I would have fed them also with the finest wheat-flour; * and with honey out of the stony rock would I have satisfied thee.
