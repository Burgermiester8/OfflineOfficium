138:1 O LORD, thou hast searched me óut, and knówn me. * Thou knowest my down-sitting, and mine uprising; † thou understandest my thóughts long befóre.
138:3 Thou art about my path, and abóut my béd; * and art acquainted with áll my wáys.
138:4 For lo, there is not a wórd in my tóngue, * but thou, O LORD, knówest it altogéther.
138:5 Thou hast beset me behínd and befóre, * and laid thine hánd upón me.
138:6 Such knowledge is too wonderful and éxcellent for mé; * I cannot attáin unto ít.
138:7 Whither shall I go then fróm thy Spírit? * or whither shall I go then fróm thy présence?
138:8 If I climb up into heaven, thóu art thére; * if I go down to hell, thou árt there álso.
138:9 If I take the wíngs of the mórning, * and remain in the uttermost párts of the séa;
138:10 Even there also shall thý hand léad me, * and thy ríght hand shall hóld me.
138:11 If I say, Peradventure the dárkness shall cóver me; * then shall my night be túrned to dáy.
138:12 Yea, the darkness is no darkness with thee, † but the night is as cléar as the dáy; * the darkness and light to thee are bóth alíke.
138:13 For my réins are thíne; * thou hast covered me in my móther’s wómb.
138:14 I will give thanks unto thee, for I am fearfully and wónderfully máde: * marvelous are thy works, and that my soul knóweth right wéll.
138:15 My bones are not híd from thée, * though I be made secretly, and fashioned benéath in the éarth.
138:16 Thine eyes did see my substance, yet béing impérfect; * and in thy book were all my mémbers wrítten;
138:17 Which day by dáy were fáshioned, * when as yet there was nóne of thém.
138:18a How dear are thy counsels unto mé, O Gód; * O how gréat is the súm of them!
138:18b If I tell them, they are more in númber than the sánd: * when I wake up, I am présent with thée.
138:19 Wilt thou not slay the wícked, O Gód? * Depart from me, ye blóodthirsty mén.
138:20 For they speak unríghteously agáinst thee; * and thine enemies take thy Náme in váin.
138:21 Do not I hate them, O LÓRD, that háte thee? * and am not I grieved with those that ríse up agáinst thee?
138:22 Yea, I háte them right sóre; * even as though they wére mine énemies.
138:23 Try me, O God, and seek the gróund of my héart; * prove me, and exámine my thóughts.
138:24 Look well if there be any way of wíckedness ín me; * and lead me in the wáy everlásting.
