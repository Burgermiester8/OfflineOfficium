24:1 UNTO thee, O LORD, will I lift up my soul; † my God, I have put my trúst in thée: * O let me not be confounded, neither let mine enemies tríumph over mé.
24:4 For all they that hope in thee shall nót be ashámed; * but such as transgress without a cause shall be pút to confúsion.
24:4 Show me thy wáys, O LÓRD, * and teach mé thy páths.
24:5 Lead me forth in thy trúth, and léarn me: * for thou art the God of my salvation; in thee hath been my hópe all the day lóng.
24:6 Call to remembrance, O LORD, thy ténder mércies, * and thy loving-kindnesses, which have been éver of óld.
24:7 O remember not the sins and offénces of my yóuth; * but according to thy mercy think thou upon me, O LÓRD, for thy góodness.
24:8 Gracious and ríghteous is the LÓRD; * therefore, will he teach sínners in the wáy.
24:9 Them that are meek shall he gúide in júdgment; * and such as are gentle, them shall he léarn his wáy.
24:10 All the paths of the LORD are mércy and trúth, * unto such as keep his cóvenant and his téstimonies.
24:11 For thy Name’s sáke, O LÓRD, * be merciful unto my sín; for it is gréat.
24:12 What man is he that féareth the LÓRD? * him shall he teach in the wáy that he shall chóose.
24:13 His soul shall dwéll at éase, * and his seed shall inhérit the lánd.
24:14 The secret of the LORD is among thém that fear hím; * and he will shów them his cóvenant.
24:15 Mine eyes are ever looking únto the LÓRD; * for he shall pluck my feet óut of the nét.
24:16 Turn thee unto me, and have mércy upon mé; * for I am désolate, and in mísery.
24:17 The sorrows of my héart are enlárged: * O bring thou me óut of my tróubles.
24:18 Look upon my advérsity and mísery, * and forgive me áll my sín.
24:19 Consider mine enemies, how mány they áre; * and they bear a tyrannous háte against mé.
24:20 O keep my soul, and delíver mé: * let me not be confounded, for I have put my trúst in thée.
24:21 Let perfectness and righteous dealing wáit upon mé; * for my hope hath béen in thée.
24:22 Deliver Ísrael, O Gód, * out of áll his tróubles.
