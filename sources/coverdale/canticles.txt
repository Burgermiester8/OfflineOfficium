# The Magnificat and the Benedictus in the Prayer Book's English, as given for
# this project. Added to the text as given: the verse numbers (Luke 1, as the
# Latin files number them), the * where the Prayer Book points the verse with a
# colon, and the + where the sign of the cross is made (where the project's own
# English files have it). A Prayer Book verse that holds two of Luke's is
# numbered by the first (46 is 46-47, 54 is 54-55); one Luke divides in two
# takes a and b (48a, 48b).
#
# tools/make_coverdale.py copies each [PsalmN] below into that psalm's file.

[Psalm232]
(Canticle of the Blessed Virgin * Luke 1:46-55)
1:46 My soul + doth magnify the Lord * and my spirit hath rejoiced in God my Saviour.
1:48a For he hath regarded * the lowliness of his handmaiden.
1:48b For behold, from henceforth * all generations shall call me blessed.
1:49 For he that is mighty hath magnified me * and holy is his Name.
1:50 And his mercy is on them that fear him * throughout all generations.
1:51 He hath shewed strength with his arm * he hath scattered the proud in the imagination of their hearts.
1:52 He hath put down the mighty from their seat * and hath exalted the humble and meek.
1:53 He hath filled the hungry with good things * and the rich he hath sent empty away.
1:54 He remembering his mercy hath holpen his servant Israel * as he promised to our forefathers, Abraham and his seed for ever.

[Psalm231]
(Canticle of Zacharias * Luke 1:68-79)
1:68 BLESSED be the Lord + God of Israel * for he hath visited and redeemed his people;
1:69 And hath raised up a mighty salvation for us * in the house of his servant David;
1:70 As he spake by the mouth of his holy Prophets * which have been since the world began;
1:71 That we should be saved from our enemies * and from the hand of all that hate us.
1:72 To perform the mercy promised to our forefathers * and to remember his holy Covenant;
1:73 To perform the oath which he sware to our forefather Abraham * that he would give us;
1:74 That we being delivered out of the hand of our enemies * might serve him without fear;
1:75 In holiness and righteousness before him * all the days of our life.
1:76 And thou, Child, shalt be called the Prophet of the Highest * for thou shalt go before the face of the Lord to prepare his ways;
1:77 To give knowledge of salvation unto his people * for the remission of their sins,
1:78 Through the tender mercy of our God * whereby the day-spring from on high hath visited us;
1:79 To give light to them that sit in darkness, and in the shadow of death * and to guide our feet into the way of peace.
